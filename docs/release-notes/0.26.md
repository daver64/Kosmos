# Kosmos 0.26

## Multiplayer weapons
- Other players' beams and sparklies are now shown in your game, with their sounds, impact flashes and lights. Shots from another player's ship are for show and do no damage.
- The multiplayer protocol changed (version 2): a 0.26 game can only be joined by 0.26 players.

## Weapons
- New scenario 47, *Earth: weapons range*: the Deltaglider with an Argo and a Wren a few hundred metres ahead as targets.

## KosmosLink
- A new opt-in `weapon` event tells a program when a weapon hits a ship: the ship's index, whether the shield or the hull took it, the weapon id and the ship's health.
- 3D drawing with depth on is now hidden by terrain relief and by the real extents of ships and buildings, not just bounding spheres.
- `/ships/ship[N]/health` is now published beside `shield`.
- The SDK manual describes weapons, the `fire` request and the new event; the README roadmap and the flight manual are brought up to date.

## Other
- The frame rate and frame time are published as `/sim/frame-rate` and `/sim/frame-time` and shown on the Info screen (**I**).
- Every slider has arrow buttons for single-step changes, such as the launch heading.
- Physics now runs in fixed 1/240 s steps at 4x warp and below, whatever the frame rate, so a launch behaves the same on a slow or a fast machine and a slow frame can no longer make a rocket on its pad unstable. If the machine cannot keep up, the simulation runs slower than real time instead of taking bigger steps.
