# Kosmos 0.22

- Changed: on foot, Space now jumps and Ctrl crouches (they were the other way round); in zero gravity Space moves you up and Ctrl down.
- New: on foot, every building's door is marked on screen ("DOOR <name> <distance>") up to 300 m away, and "Press Enter to go into <name>" appears when you are close enough. The door reach is a little more generous.
- Fixed: jumping while pressed against a building (or any solid) threw you away at over 100 m/s, because the planet's spin was being cancelled along with your own motion.
- Changed: nights on Mars and other thin-air worlds are darker; the surface fill light after dusk is much lower.
- Fixed: the base object types `solar_array` and `solar_windmill` stood on edge with half the panel under the ground (and the windmill looked like a satellite). Both are now tilted ground racks on posts, as at Gale Crater.
- Changed: the Gale building panels are laid out in three columns (status, controls, power bus) with section headings. Gauges now show their reading inside the bar, and switches carry their power lamp inside the button. For modders, a `label` control with a width draws a rule under its text.
- New: on foot, the ` (backquote) key switches between mouselook and a free pointer for the menus, for desktops where Alt-click is taken by the window manager.
- Fixed: the suit HUD altitude and vertical speed flickered between values when standing on the ground. Within 0.2 m of the ground the altitude now reads 0.0.
- New: the Tranquility Base (Moon), Mare Frigoris outpost (Moon) and Caloris outpost (Mercury) now have working buildings you can enter, like Gale Crater's: a habitat, a power plant and a comms mast at each, and an ISRU plant at Tranquility. They come from a new `outpost-base` addon.
- New: building interfaces. A building's panel can now be viewed as a 3D board (press C inside a building: chase, 2D panel, then 3D) with clickable controls, and can use a background bitmap via `layout.image`. The Gale and outpost habitats have one. For modders, a building may name its block `"interface"` instead of `"cockpit"`; `layout.position`, `size` and `tilt` place the 3D board.
