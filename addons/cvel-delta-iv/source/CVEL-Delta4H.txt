CVEL Delta 4 Heavy  Version 1.0

(c) 13/12/2004 Ervins Reinverts (a.k.a erv) ervins@gmail.com

Delta IV meshes are by Alex Hohlov (STS-107), used with permission.

Many programming ideas taken from other CVEL vessels code,
particulary Ariane, Fregat, Proton-K by David Henderson et al.


Addon is CVEL based, as one could guess :>
Payloads are described with GPDS (see http://projectganymede.sf.net/cve-lite/).


The rocket mostly follows (at least tries to!) the launch sequence
as described here:
http://www.spaceflightnow.com/delta/d310/041201launchtimeline.html
(including throttling the engines)

It does it until second stage ignition followed by fairing jettison
ten seconds later. After that, you are on your own!

And no, it does not have auto. ;-)

 =========
  K e y s
 =========

J - jettison payload(s), one by one
0 - prematurely jettison fairings (normally done automatically)
1 - prematurely separate stages (normally done automatically)
9 - some debug info (not too useful though :> )


Good luck!
