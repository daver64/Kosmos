# Kosmos 0.20

- New: the CVEL Shuttle-A can carry detachable cargo pods (the Orbiter Shuttle-A payload container, 20 t), carried as four pods, two pairs on either side of the spine (six cargo bays). Press U to release them and fly back to a bay to dock one again. Scenarios 12 (Helsinki pad) and 13 (400 km orbit) start with four pods attached. The pod's parachutes are not modelled.
- New for modders: a scenario ship can start docked with `"dock": {"to", "port", "own_port"}`.
- New: a Shuttle-A fuel pod (20 t of propellant) for the two spare aft bays. Docked, it tops up the shuttle's tank automatically. View > Fuel transfer chooses Automatic, Off, Pump to docked ships or Pump from docked ships. Scenarios 12 and 13 now carry two fuel pods.
- Fixed: a ship carrying docked mass (such as the Shuttle-A with pods) sank into the ground; the gear springs now stiffen with the extra weight.
- New for modders: `"fuel_pod": true` in a ship JSON makes it feed docked ships; the mode is the property `/dock/transfer` (auto, off, give, take), with `/dock/partner-fuel` and `/dock/partner-capacity` in kg.
- Fixed: ships placed on a base's launch or landing pad sank into it. The ground under every pad is now raised to the concrete, and the launch pad's flame-trench is a level plate, so ships rest on the pad.
- New: weather. Every planet with an atmosphere has 64 weather stations that issue METARs (procedural, from noise; Mars has dust, Titan methane rain). Wind pushes aircraft and rockets and grows with height, rain wets the runway and cuts tyre and brake grip, and fog thickens as visibility drops. Rain streaks the screen below the cloud base and a weather line shows wind, rain, cloud, visibility and the nearest station.
- New: live weather for Earth. View > Graphics > Live (Earth, NOAA) downloads real METARs from aviationweather.gov at most once an hour, from 64 stations spread around the world, and applies them while the simulated date is within three hours of today. Windows uses the built-in WinHTTP, so nothing extra is needed; Linux uses libcurl. Weather can be turned off in the same menu.
- New for modders: `/environment/*` properties (wind-east, wind-north, wind-speed, wind-from, rain, wetness, cloud-cover, cloud-base, cloud-top, visibility, temperature, pressure, station, metar, live); `data/weather/bodies.json` can override a body's climate.
