# Texture credits

Many planet and moon maps in `textures/` and `textures-hires/` are made from texture packs for
[Celestia](https://celestiaproject.space/), with individual authors and terms.
Kosmos converts, resizes and recombines them (see `tools/celestia_import.py`, `tools/ctx_import.py`).
The provenance and redistribution terms of every bundled map have not yet been verified.
Keep this file with any copy of the textures.

## Local optional imports (not shipped)

Unverified `data/textures/*_normal.jpg` planetary normal maps have been
removed from the Git index and excluded from application packages. Local
copies can still be used by a development installation; without a normal
map, the corresponding packaged surface has no normal-map relief.

Asteroid textures and meshes (Ida, Gaspra, Kleopatra, Toutatis) come from the Orbiter Main Belt Asteroids add-on. They are not redistributed. `tools/mainbelt_import.py` converts a local copy into `data/textures-hires/`, which is git-ignored.

High-resolution Earth clouds (`earth_clouds.dds`, `earth_clouds/`), Earth night lights, and Mercury, Titan, Io, Europa, Enceladus, Phobos and Vesta (`<name>.dds`) may be converted locally by `tools/orbiter_import.py` and `tools/orbiter_tiles.py` from Orbiter 2016 high-resolution packs (`import/orbiter/`). They are git-ignored; their redistribution terms have not been established, and they are not in the procedural-only pack.
