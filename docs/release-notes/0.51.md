# Kosmos 0.51

- Optional nonspherical gravity now uses spherical-harmonic fields for Mercury, Venus, Earth, Moon, Mars, Ceres and Vesta, using the same datasets and degree limits as Orbiter's configurations. The simulated bodies' ephemerides and orientations remain approximations, so trajectories need not match Orbiter exactly. Rails, time skips and Keplerian orbit predictions still omit these perturbations.
- Pioneer 10 and Voyager's Jupiter scenarios start with their dishes aimed at Earth. A spacecraft Earth-communications attitude hold tracks Earth during physics flight; it does not simulate a radio link or steer on rails.
- Experimental target autoland can aim at a base's pad from nearby or plan an orbit-to-base plane change and powered descent, with Ares on Mars or the Moon and Shuttle-A on suitable bodies. Orbital success is within 3 km of the base centre, not necessarily on the pad; it requires spherical gravity and weather off on bodies with weather. The approximate forecast checks fuel but cannot guarantee a landing or avoid obstacles. Local approaches descend faster above 1000 m; autoland blocks time skips and aborts if the body or stage changes.
- Orbital base autoland shows the time to plane burn during the wait, recheck and alignment. Planning and checking run at 1x; alignment, burn and descent permit up to 4x physics warp.
- Nuclear explosions, beam weapons and thunder now use synthesised sounds rather than recordings. Sound credits identify the bundled XRSound effects.
- Removed unverified planet and moon imagery, including Earth night lights. The bundled Moon, Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune maps are now fictional procedural approximations; some smaller bodies use generated fallback textures. Optional scenery can replace them.
- Titan, Delta IV, Atlas and Proton-K rocket visuals now use simplified procedural shapes instead of imported models with unresolved redistribution terms or compliance. Their grouped fairing segments appear as one piece and visually detach together.
- Energia-M also uses simplified procedural shapes; its fairing is drawn as one piece and detaches with the first fairing stage.
- Buran and its Energia launcher use procedural placeholders instead of imported models with unverified terms. Buran's generic aircraft placeholder does not visually animate its landing gear or bay doors.
- Centaur G and Transtage also use procedural placeholders; Transtage's attached probe visual is simplified.
- Ares Spider uses a procedural placeholder instead of its imported model; the landing legs no longer appear or animate, though touchdown contacts are unchanged.
- Salyut and Lunar Salyut use procedural placeholders in place of imported models with unverified terms; solar arrays are not shown.
- Dragonfly uses a procedural placeholder and a plain cockpit panel, with hover tooltips for some unlabelled controls. Its latch and antenna controls no longer animate visually.
- Luna E3 uses a simplified procedural placeholder in place of its imported model.
- ISS and its nuclear variant use a procedural placeholder; the docking ports and solar arrays do not match its simplified appearance.
- Mir and its nuclear variant also use a simplified procedural placeholder without solar arrays.
- Wheel uses a simplified procedural ring and hub in place of its imported mesh; the spokes and surface details are not shown.
- Removed converted Orbiter scenery meshes and textures pending asset-specific rights verification. Kennedy now uses a procedural launch pad and tower and the NASA-derived VAB; the launch pad deck is lower than before.
- Deltaglider variants use a generic aircraft placeholder and plain panels instead of artwork with unresolved terms. Gear, hatch and airbrake no longer animate visually; the original virtual cockpit is unavailable and some panel controls rely on hover tooltips.
- Shuttle-A variants and their detachable pods use procedural placeholders and plain panels. Their original virtual cockpit, landing gear and swivelling engine pod animations are not shown; the controls and flight systems remain.
