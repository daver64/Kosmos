# Kosmos 0.43

- Quantum jump is now done through portals: every system has a Quantum Portal ring in orbit of its star (about 13 AU out in Sol). Dial a star from the galaxy map or View > Quantum portal within 100 km of it, then fly through the open disc to leave from the destination's portal. The hyperjump drive requirement and the choice of arrival world are gone. New scenario "CVEL: Shuttle-A at the Sol quantum portal", ship key `portal`, scenario key `portal_m`, option `--test-dial`; `--test-jump-body` is removed.
- Invented worlds no longer stall the game on arrival: a 512-wide image is shown at once and the larger far-view images are generated in the background by two helper processes (nearest world first) and swapped in as they finish. Cached images are reused.
- New Fuel MFD: propellant by stage and RCS, burn rate and endurance, and fuel transfer buttons (AUTO, OFF, GIVE, TAKE) when docked.
- New ship CVEL Shuttle-A (Nuclear), a Shuttle-A with a 12 kW fission reactor. The Shuttle-A portal scenario now flies it as an expedition ship with two fuel pods and four life support pods docked.
- Navigation lights and strobes now start off on every ship (Y turns them on; cabin lamps still start on).
- Fixed the Shuttle-A family's 3D cockpit MFD screens being upside down; a cockpit can set `"vc_mfd_flip": true` for a model whose screen UVs are the other way up.
- The Deltaglider (and the nuclear one) now has Orbiter's three hover engines: one forward and two aft, in the same layout and thrust ratio, with the same total hover thrust as before.
- Twin levers (the Shuttle-A pod thrust pair, on the 2D panel and in the 3D cockpit) now move together when you grab between them or on their inner halves; only grabbing the outer half of one lever moves it alone.
- The Deltaglider elevator trim now works for level flight: the cockpit trim buttons (2D panel and 3D cockpit, with their directions corrected so the upper one is nose up) and new keys Ctrl+Page Up / Ctrl+Page Down trim nose up / down while held, and the trim has more authority so it can hold level flight.
- Fixed rocket ascent autopilots (Saturn V, MLV3, V-23L and the other CVEL launchers) falling out of the sky under time warp during a burn: scripts now get simulated time rather than frame time, so the pitch programme and staging timers keep pace at 10x.
- New NASA ships: Pioneer 10 and Voyager probes (from the NASA imports). Each has an aft payload port so it can ride on a launcher, real masses and hydrazine thrusters, RTGs that decay with their real half-lives, and every instrument, transmitter and computer as its own load.
- The F1 Ship systems window now has a checkbox per load, so instruments can be switched off individually to shed power as the RTGs fade; scenarios can set `systems.rtg_age_years` to start a probe part-way through its mission.
- New scenarios `NASA: Pioneer 10 inbound to Jupiter` and `NASA: Voyager inbound to Jupiter`, dated to the real flybys, with the RTGs already aged.
- New ships imported from the Orbiter CVEL add-ons: the Ariane 5 G, ES and ECA launchers and the ATV cargo ferry (CVEL Ariane 5), and the Buran orbiter with the original four-booster Energia (CVEL Buran). The launcher autopilot (P) flies all the Ariane 5s and the Energia. Buran has animated retractable landing gear (G) and payload bay doors (View > Animations), and, like the Deltaglider, its MFDs offer both the orbital and the aerodynamic modes. The Energia core now has its four RD-0120 engines.
- New ships imported from the Orbiter CVEL Titans add-on (CVEL Titan): Titan I, II, IIIB, IIIC, IIID, IIIE, 34D, IVA and IVB, with their solid motors, Transtage, Centaur D and Agena upper stages, and fairings. The launcher autopilot (P) flies all nine to orbit. Their meshes were cleaned of coplanar duplicate triangles (tools/obj_zfight.py). The Titan C, Cluster and IIIM variants are not included. One scenario per ship under `CVEL: Titan ...`.
- The Transfer MFD shows the burn time: Burn 1 time at the current thrust, and while you are burning (by hand or with AUTO) the minutes and dV left, to compare with the Fuel MFD endurance.
- ORBITCOMP shows the burn time for the planned dV on the Plan pages (ejection, return, Hohmann burn 1) and the manoeuvre node. Once the engines fire it counts the dV delivered and shows the burn time and dV left.
- New scenarios `CVEL: Ariane 5 ES, Soyuz TMA, Kourou`, `CVEL: Ariane 5 ECA, Kourou` and `CVEL: Buran on Energia, Baikonur`.
- Ariane 5 upper-stage, core and fairing meshes had coplanar duplicate triangles (z-fighting); cleaned with tools/obj_zfight.py. The autopilot now uses a lofted climb for the Ariane 5s; the ECA reaches orbit with it, but the ES scenario now carries a Soyuz TMA instead of the ATV, which was too heavy for the ES upper stage, and reaches a 195 x 829 km orbit.
- Ships that start docked to another (the Deltaglider on the Energia-M, Buran on Energia) now start with their landing gear retracted.
- The docking and touchdown sounds no longer play when a scenario starts; event sounds wait two seconds while the start-up state settles.
- Removed a libpng "known incorrect sRGB profile" warning by stripping the colour profile from the interior floor texture.
