## Kosmos 0.15

### Interface
- MFD and panel instruments now suit the ship: aircraft no longer offer the orbital displays, and spacecraft no longer show the airspeed, vertical speed, turn and heading gauges. The Skylark's Navigation panel now carries an HSI instead of the Orbit display.
- The COM/NAV and other nav displays show short ICAO-style idents (e.g. `EGCP VOR`, `EGCP ILS 1R`) instead of long names that overran the screen.

### Scenery
- Runways, roads, launch pads and landing pads now have a tiling asphalt texture instead of flat colour; painted markings still show through, slightly weathered.

### Planets
- New, sharper maps for Uranus, Neptune and Pluto, and Pluto's moon Charon is now in the Solar System with its own map. Pluto and Charon have relief shading.

### For addon authors
- MFD modes in an addon manifest can set `"domain"` (`space` or `atmosphere`), and a ship's `cockpit.mfd_filter` (`auto`, `atmosphere`, `space`, `none`) controls which are offered. Use `none` for a spaceplane.
- Bases take an optional `"icao"` code (made up from the id if absent), and navaids an optional `"ident"`.

## OrbitComp
- New **OrbitComp** orbital-computer MFD (inspired by Orbiter's TransX): orbit plot with target and closest approach, manoeuvre nodes (T+, Pe, Ap, AN, DN) with post-burn orbit and burn time, and Hohmann/ejection planning that can be loaded into a node.
- New `--set PATH=VALUE` option.

## Cameras
- New **Camera editor** (View menu, or the button in the F2 window): build your own cameras, saved between sessions.
  - *Orbit* cameras circle a ship or a planet or moon and always look at it; right-drag swings round, `[` `]` zoom, and an optional spin turns it by itself.
  - *Mounted* cameras sit at an offset on a ship or body and face a set direction, or look at another ship or body.
- An orbiting **External** camera is provided, and `C` now cycles on through your cameras after the chase view and cockpits. They are also listed in F2.
- New `--user-camera N` and `--camera-editor` options.

## Loading and fixes
- New **loading screen** with a dim Martian-haze backdrop that says what is being loaded.
- Fixed the libpng "iCCP" warning printed at startup.
- After staging, a ship now rotates about the centre of mass of the stages that remain, rather than a point left behind in space, so turning an upper stage no longer swings it through the stage that just left.
- Earth's night-side city lights were mirrored east-west, so they appeared over the sea instead of the cities; they now line up with the land.
- The on-screen text is now off when Kosmos starts (press `I` to show it, or use `--show-info`), and the "Renderer =" label is gone.
- The download is now about 535 MB. The high-resolution textures (the sharper planet maps, the Earth surface tiles, and the specular and normal maps) are a separate optional download, `kosmos-hires.tar.gz` (1.2 GB). Unpack it inside the Kosmos folder with `tar -xf kosmos-hires.tar.gz`.
- Fixed stars and the sky going black when the nearest body is far away (close to a distant star, for example). The near plane could end up beyond the sky, or even beyond the far plane.
- Fixed the Earth cloud layer being sliced off along a straight line across the view in low orbit (below about 300 km).
- The website now works properly on phones: a collapsible contents list, wrapping menu and single-column layouts.
