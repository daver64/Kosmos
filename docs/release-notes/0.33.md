# Kosmos 0.33

## Changes
- New **Edit > Performance settings** window in the sim, next to Settings. It gathers the launcher's graphics options (render style, clouds, weather, terrain detail, frame-rate limit, light radius, Earth relief), the scenery (trees, buildings and rocks) and its density, and the textures, including a new saved "Largest texture" setting. These moved out of the F3 Settings window.
- New saved scenery draw-distance setting (25 to 200 %) next to the scatter density, to trade distance for frame rate.
- New **Cloud style** option in Performance settings: Simple (the single deck, still the default), Layers (every reported METAR layer, up to four, in the sky) or Volumetric (ray-marched 3D towers on cumulus and storm cloud; heavy on the GPU). Layers and Volumetric also grey the view while you fly inside thick cloud.
- Fixed thunder and lightning continuing at 30 km and above. Storms now stop a few km above the highest cloud top.
- Fixed clouds being sliced off along a line across the view as you climb: the near plane (and the Earth cloud shell) now leave room for off-axis cloud.
- Lightning now draws textured bolts from `data/textures/lightning/` (up to three at once) and thunder plays the recording `data/sounds/lightning.wav`. Thunder is rarer (a strike every 12 to 42 seconds at full storm) and its volume and pitch follow the strike's distance: near strikes crack loud, far ones rumble quietly. A faint far rumble is skipped while a louder one is still rolling.
- `--performance-window` opens it at start.
- Internal cleanup with no change in behaviour: more long functions are split into smaller ones (ship landing and systems update, the power bus, addon loading, tagged-vessel discovery, terrain and clutter preparation, KosmosLink 3D overlays, the base editor's object tab, scenario start-up, body and world drawing, and ship control handling).
