# Kosmos 0.47

- KosmosMission carries payload ships: add any ship, several kinds and counts (a Dragonfly and a dozen probes), at their full mass, and it checks they fit the launcher's payload volumes. Ships declare volumes with `payload_bays` (cylinders or boxes) and their own size with `payload_size` in `ship.json`. The fit check is conservative. Without a declared volume the mass counts but nothing is checked.
- Payload stacks: build a 2-3 stage stack (for example Centaur G, Fregat, a probe) with Add to stack / Add stack. It counts as one payload, its mass and size are summed, and its own delta-v is added to the budget.
- When you save a mission or start the game, the first unit in each bay is docked to the launcher in the scenario (a stack docks bottom to top). A bay names its launcher port with `"port"`; anything that did not fit or had no free port is listed in the scenario description as not placed.
- All the CVEL launchers (Saturn V variants, Ariane 5, Atlas, Delta IV, Proton, the Titans, Buran, Energia, Energia-M, Centaur, Fregat and the Shuttle-A pods) now declare payload bays. The sizes are estimates, not measured. The Saturn V, MLV3 and V-23L have new "Payload" variants (no service or command module) with a Payload port on top of the S-IVB and a 22 m payload section, which is where KosmosMission docks their payloads; the Apollo versions carry the CSM and declare no payload space. The Ares Spider gained an Aft port so it can be carried.
- KosmosMission's ship list now includes the variants listed in an add-on's `addon.json` `"ships"` (Titan models, Ariane 5 ES and ECA, Energia, Soyuz, Shuttle-A pods), not only each add-on's `ship.json`.
- The launcher has a KosmosMission button on the Play page. KosmosMission can start Kosmos itself (with the mission as the scenario, via `--link`) when you press **Start Kosmos with this mission** or **To game** and no game is running; it then sends the leg once the game answers.
- The tools that ship with Kosmos (KosmosMission, KosmosMap, KosmosCAD, KosmosAV, ShipEdit) are now allowed to connect without approval. Other native addons still need allowing under View > Native addons.
- KosmosMission has a **Guide** button and a web-page walk-through of a complete mission: assemble a stack, fly the ascent, then a Lambert or Hohmann leg.
- Fixed a GTK deprecation warning (`G_APPLICATION_FLAGS_NONE`) in the Windows build of KosmosMission.
- The Ares Spider's four landing legs now fold in against the body (Landing legs (stow) in the animation controls). KosmosMission sizes it with the legs stowed (about 7.6 m by 8.8 m), so it fits the Saturn V payload bays. A scenario starts it with the legs deployed.
- The Apollo Command Module (the separate ship) gained Aft and Nose docking ports; without them a stack that included it was left at the launch site instead of docked. KosmosMission now leaves out, and notes in the scenario description, any payload ship that has no docking port.
- Parachutes no longer deploy by themselves on the pad or while a capsule is stacked on a rocket (the Apollo Command Module opened its canopy at once on the ground). The automatic deployment is now armed only after the ship has been above the chute's `arm_altitude` (default three times `auto_altitude`, at least 20 km); Delete still deploys by hand.
- Low orbit over Earth: the cloud layer no longer smears into blue streaks above the horizon. Clouds fade out where the view grazes the planet (needs a simlib with `PlanetTilesOptions::limb_fade`).
- Ship skins: a scenario ship can set `"skin"` to a folder in the ship's `Skins/` folder (the Deltaglider has Shuttle_BRI, CAN, FRA and USA); the scenario editor has a Skin selector. The CVEL Deltaglider, Chippenham scenario now starts the Nuclear Deltaglider in the Shuttle_BRI skin.
- The Deltaglider's cockpit seen from outside is no longer a blank white shape: the interior materials were self-lit at 0.8, which blew the textures out to white. They are now lit normally (the virtual cockpit has its own model and is unchanged).
- Cloud style Layers and Volumetric: the sky cloud layers fade out as you climb above their tops (from 3 km above to gone at 20 km above), so they no longer show as streaks along the horizon from high altitude. The planet's own cloud map shows the weather from there.
- CVEL Deltaglider (Nuclear): a stronger heat-resistant airframe (limits raised from 1900/1200 K to 3200/2400 K), so its engines no longer burn out when it accelerates through the upper atmosphere.
