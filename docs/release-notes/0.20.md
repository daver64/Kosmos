# Kosmos 0.20

- New: the CVEL Shuttle-A can carry detachable cargo pods (the Orbiter Shuttle-A payload container, 20 t), carried as four pods, two pairs on either side of the spine (six cargo bays). Press U to release them and fly back to a bay to dock one again. Scenarios 12 (Helsinki pad) and 13 (400 km orbit) start with four pods attached. The pod's parachutes are not modelled.
- New for modders: a scenario ship can start docked with `"dock": {"to", "port", "own_port"}`.
- New: a Shuttle-A fuel pod (20 t of propellant) for the two spare aft bays. Docked, it tops up the shuttle's tank automatically. View > Fuel transfer chooses Automatic, Off, Pump to docked ships or Pump from docked ships. Scenarios 12 and 13 now carry two fuel pods.
- Fixed: a ship carrying docked mass (such as the Shuttle-A with pods) sank into the ground; the gear springs now stiffen with the extra weight.
- New for modders: `"fuel_pod": true` in a ship JSON makes it feed docked ships; the mode is the property `/dock/transfer` (auto, off, give, take), with `/dock/partner-fuel` and `/dock/partner-capacity` in kg.
- Fixed: ships placed on a base's launch or landing pad sank into it. The ground under every pad is now raised to the concrete, and the launch pad's flame-trench is a level plate, so ships rest on the pad.
- New: weather. Every planet with an atmosphere has 64 weather stations that issue METARs (procedural, from noise; Mars has dust, Titan methane rain). Wind acts on every ship (rockets and landers as well as aircraft), each feeling the wind at its own position, and grows with height, rain wets the runway and cuts tyre and brake grip, and fog thickens as visibility drops. A layer of cloud forms at the reported cloud base below 100 km and drifts with the wind. Rain falls as streaks in the air around you, below the cloud base, slanting with the wind and your own speed, and a weather line shows wind, rain, cloud, visibility and the nearest station.
- New: live weather for Earth. View > Graphics > Live (Earth, NOAA) downloads real METARs from aviationweather.gov at most once an hour, from 64 stations spread around the world, and applies them while the simulated date is within three hours of today. Windows uses the built-in WinHTTP, so nothing extra is needed; Linux uses libcurl. Weather can be turned off in the same menu.
- New for modders: `/environment/*` properties (wind-east, wind-north, wind-speed, wind-from, rain, wetness, cloud-cover, cloud-base, cloud-top, visibility, temperature, pressure, station, metar, live); `data/weather/bodies.json` can override a body's climate.
- New: weather presets (clear, scattered cloud, overcast, rain, fog, thunderstorm) and a custom METAR line you can type in, in View > Graphics and the launcher. Thunderstorms have lightning: a flash, a jagged bolt from the cloud base, and thunder that arrives after the delay sound takes to travel.
- New: the launcher's Graphics page has weather settings: weather on or off, real Earth weather, and rain.
- New for modders: a scenario can set `"weather": "<METAR line>"` to fix the weather everywhere (see scenario 46, Skylark in a storm).
- New: an Autopilot menu with two windows. Aircraft: heading hold, altitude or vertical-speed hold, an autothrottle, and NAV/APPR modes that fly an ILS localizer and glideslope. Spacecraft: ascent target orbit and heading, attitude hold modes and throttle. P now engages the aircraft autopilot on any aircraft, holding its current heading and altitude.
- New for modders: a scenario can set `"throttle"`, `"autopilot"`, `"mfd_left"` and `"mfd_right"`; the aircraft modes are `/autopilot/ac/*` and the ascent settings `/autopilot/sc/*`.
- Docs: the flight manual has a full Weather section (sources, presets, custom METAR, lightning, effects on flight, settings) and a rewritten Autopilot section for the aircraft and spacecraft windows, with new screenshots. The modders' manual has new Weather and Autopilots sections (stations, METAR format, every `/environment/*`, `/sim/settings/weather-*`, `/autopilot/ac/*` and `/autopilot/sc/*` property, and the new scenario keys). The gallery has weather and autopilot shots.
- Changed: the H key on an aircraft now toggles the surface HUD (airspeed, altitude, heading tape, pitch ladder) instead of cycling through the orbital and docking HUDs. A ship can choose its own list with `cockpit.hud_modes` in `ship.json`, for example `["orbit", "surface", "docking"]` for a spaceplane.
- New: the Skylark has a new textured model with lights moved to match, and animated landing gear (legs fold up and the wheel covers open and close).
- Fixed: scenario 20, the Skylark ILS approach, now starts level at 5000 ft and 200 kt, five miles out on the Chippenham approach in daylight (14 October 1964, 12:00), with the engines running, the autopilot on, the HSI on the left MFD and VOR on the right, both tuned to Chippenham. Before, the aircraft started low and dived into the ground.
- New: scenarios can start with the autopilot on, a throttle setting and chosen MFD modes (`"autopilot"`, `"throttle"`, `"mfd_left"`, `"mfd_right"`).
- New: scenery on the ground. Trees, shrubs, houses and rocks on Earth follow the real land cover (forest, scrub, farmland, towns), within 50 km of a base or of your ship when it is below 5 km. Mars, the Moon, Mercury and other airless or thin-aired worlds get rocks and boulders, and only a handful of pressure domes, habitat tubes, containers, tanks and rovers around each base; the rocks run right through the base. Invented worlds with air get procedural alien plants in a colour palette of their own. Settings (F3) has a switch and a density slider; `--no-clutter` turns it off.
- New for modders: `--perf` prints the frame rate and ground-scatter CPU time. The Earth land cover ships as a gzipped cache (about 26 MB) that is unpacked on first use; `scripts/pack-landclass.sh` and `scripts/pack-devdata.sh` build it and a developer data pack. The modders' manual has a Scenery clutter section.
