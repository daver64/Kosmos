# Kosmos 0.40

- New CVEL Energia-M launcher (scenario 27), with autopilot support. It carries a nuclear Deltaglider piggyback on its core, docked at the new "Cradle" port; undock with Ctrl+D after the core burns out.
- The Delta IV Heavy (scenario 04) now has a two-piece payload fairing that jettisons after the boosters separate, and carries a Centaur G with a Luna E3 probe docked on top (the Centaur-with-probe orbit scenario, 03, docks its probe the same way).
- Staging: the two Delta IV fairing halves go on a single press (`"with_next": true` on a stage in `ship.json`). On a ship's last stage the stage key releases its docked payload (rearmost first) and switches to it, so a Delta IV can stage all the way down to its probe.
- Launch pads: the levelled deck under a pad is now 15 m across so tall rockets stand square; Energia-M and Delta IV Heavy no longer sink into the Kennedy pad or topple.
- Rockets on a pad now stand with their roof to the west, so pitching up (keypad 2) tips the nose east and a gravity turn gains the planet's spin, on any world. `pad_turn` in a ship's `ship.json` swings it from there.
- The base editor has a "Pad height" field (`pad.deck` in the base JSON) for how high a ship stands above the levelled ground, and it now opens on the base nearest your ship; choose another from "Base" if you want one.
- New spaceport bases: Baikonur, Plesetsk, Vostochny, Kourou, Vandenberg, Wallops, Starbase, Jiuquan, Taiyuan, Xichang, Wenchang, Tanegashima, Sriharikota, Alcantara, Mahia, Palmachim, Semnan and Naro.
- New airport bases with real-heading runways and ILS: Hornafjordur (BIHN), Keflavik (BIKF), Vagar (EKVG), Miami (KMIA), Paris Charles de Gaulle (LFPG), Rome Fiumicino (LIRF) and Berlin Brandenburg (EDDB).
- New outer-system bases on Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Enceladus, Triton and Pluto. They and the interstellar depots are powered by new reactor and RTG buildings; inner bases keep solar arrays.
- Off-Earth worlds now have only rocks as procedural scenery; habitats appear only at the authored bases.
- The tricorder now shows its readout on its own screen instead of a blank green square.
- OrbitComp's Hohmann and ejection windows counted the wrong way round (the wait grew as the window approached); fixed. The Delta IV Heavy scenario now starts on 18 May 2003, a few days before the Earth-Mars window.
- A crashed ship (such as a spent Energia core) no longer holds time warp at 4x for every other ship.
- New thumbnails for scenarios 03, 04 and 27.
- The Shuttle-A and Shuttle-AQ now carry a 24 m2 solar array and 12 kWh, 6 kW batteries, enough to run the ice miner, electrolyser and refinery together; before, starting resource processing (scenario 26) tripped a power shortage.
- Time warp on the ground now goes all the way to 100000x (it stopped at 100x) while every ship sits still and landed, so resource processing can run at pace; the ships are held to the ground while warping and released when you slow down.
