# Kosmos 0.41

- Every base building (habitats, power plants, comms masts, ISRU plants at Tranquility, Mare Frigoris, Caloris and Gale) now has a walkable interior with an airlock hatch and its control panel inside; before, only the Mare Frigoris quarters did.
- On foot, the Geology Sample readout, the pack list and the full Suit Life Support panel no longer sit on the screen. They are pages on the tricorder (environment, suit, samples, pack); click with it held to flip pages. The suit panel pops up on the HUD only when oxygen, power or pressure get low, and a compact status line shows heading, altitude and speed.
- The Deltagliders no longer carry the ICBM targeting MFD. OrbitComp's Plan page now plans a return from a moon to its planet (for example Moon to Earth: departure dV, flight time, arrival periapsis and when to burn), and the Transfer MFD points to it when the target is not in your orbit's frame.
- The Atlas III (scenario 02) now has a two-piece payload fairing that jettisons with the booster, and carries a Luna E3 probe on its Centaur; stage again to release the probe. The probe's engine plume is much smaller, so it no longer swamps the model.
- The launcher shows scenario subfolders as a nested tree (for example CVEL > CVEL-Atlas), and finds a scenario's thumbnail in any thumbs/ folder above it.
- Scenario files no longer start with a number (`dragonfly_orbit.json`, not `07_dragonfly_orbit.json`), so they can be moved between folders freely. A scenario's id is still its file name, so `--scenario dragonfly_orbit` is the new form; a launcher "last scenario" saved under an old numbered id is simply not found until you pick again.
- Three new CVEL launchers imported from the Orbiter add-ons: the Proton-K (three stages, split payload fairing and a payload port; scenario at Baikonur with a Fregat upper stage carrying a Luna E3 probe), the Saturn V-23L (four liquid boosters of two F-1s each, stretched S-IC and S-IVB) and the Saturn V MLV3 (stretched S-IC, HG-3 upper stages), each with a Kennedy scenario and ascent autopilot support. The Proton's third-stage verniers are not modelled, and the V-23L booster mass is an estimate.
- Fixed the striped z-fighting on the Saturn V stages (S-IC, S-II, S-IVB, interstage): the converted meshes had a second, coincident shell inside the hull. The duplicate triangles are removed. `tools/obj_zfight.py` finds and removes such coincident triangles in any OBJ model; the same duplicate triangles are removed from the other CVEL models (Atlas, Centaur, Delta IV, Deltaglider exterior, Energia, Proton, Shuttle-A exterior, Soyuz, Transtage, Vulcan and the Saturn V parts). The cockpit models are untouched.
- Walkable interiors (habitats and other base buildings) now have textured walls, ceilings and floors: metal panel textures with normal and specular maps for the walls and ceilings, and a bolted floor plate. The rooms are a little brighter inside too. The textures are baked by `tools/bake_interior_textures.py` and credited in `data/CREDITS-textures.md`.
- New CVEL Fregat upper stage (restartable, 5.4 t of propellant, aft and payload docking ports) imported from the Orbiter add-on; the Proton-K scenario now stacks Proton, Fregat and probe.
- Time warp up to 50x is now allowed while an engine is firing in vacuum (above the atmosphere), so long burns can be sped up; it was held at 4x before. Physics substeps are finer at high warp.
- MFDs: the see-through displays are bigger, with the page text inset between the button columns so it no longer overlaps the button boxes. Every MFD has a close button (a cross at the top right; F5 brings them back), the see-through ones gain a MNU button to pick a mode, and the mode list pages (PREV/NEXT) when more than 12 modes are available.
- Walking on foot (EVA) outside a habitat or other base building with an interior no longer passes through its walls: the person stops at the wall and slides along it, and shut doors block too. (Before, being outside the building's grid counted as already blocked, so the check never applied.)
