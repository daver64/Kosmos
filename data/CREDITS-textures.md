# Texture credits

The planet and moon maps in `textures/` and `textures-hires/` are made from texture packs for
[Celestia](https://celestiaproject.space/), whose authors released them under Creative Commons or similar open licences.
Kosmos converts, resizes and recombines them (see `tools/celestia_import.py`, `tools/ctx_import.py`); the originals belong
to their authors. Attribution is required by several of those licences, so keep this file with any copy of the textures.

Credits we can name from the packs' own notes:

- **Earth surface, specular and night-light maps, Earth normal map:** NASA Blue Marble data by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard
  Space Flight Center (<https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/>), assembled into virtual textures for Celestia by Buzz and others.
  The normal map was derived using Chris Laurel's `nm16` tool.
- **Other planets and moons** (Mercury, Moon, Mars, Jupiter and its normal map, Saturn's moons and so on): Celestia
  Motherlode and related texture packs by their individual authors, from NASA/JPL/USGS imagery and community-made maps.
  Each pack's own readme and licence is kept under `import/celestia/` in the source tree.
- **Ground detail maps** (`textures/detail_moon.jpg`, `detail_mars.jpg`): greyscale conversions of the seamless "Pebbles2" and "SmallRocks" textures from the OpenGameArt collection in `import/open-game-art/`; check the licence of the original packs there before redistributing.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Charon** (`textures/uranus.jpg`, `neptune.jpg`, `pluto.jpg`, `charon.jpg`, and the Pluto/Charon normal maps): Celestia source maps in `import/celestia/hires/` (Uranus JVV, Neptune 2k, and the Pluto/Charon maps with their bump maps); see each pack's licence.
- **Tarmac** (`textures/tarmac/ground_asphalt_synth_05.png`): the seamless asphalt texture that grains the stock runways, roads and launch/landing pads; see the licence note above.
- **Procedural planet maps** (`textures-hires/procedural/`): generated by Kosmos (`tools/gen_textures.py`).

Cloud maps, the Orbiter terrain data and any other third-party pack are credited and licensed by their own authors.
Please do not remove the credits when redistributing.

- **Deltaglider virtual cockpit** (`addons/cvel-deltaglider/models/textures/orbiter-vc/`): converted from Orbiter's stock textures, copyright Martin Schweiger, MIT licence.

The separately distributed standard Orbiter 2024 scenery packs contain the planetary
archives and the `LICENSE` from the Orbiter 2024 release (MIT, copyright Martin
Schweiger). The Earth cloud and night-light maps are converted from that release's
`Cloud.tree` and `Mask.tree`. Optional high-resolution downloads come from the
[Orbiter Forum texture mirrors](https://www.orbiter-forum.com/resources/orbiter-2016-high-resolution-texture-packs.5676/);
their source descriptions and individual credits remain applicable.
- **Rocks, nebula and tarmac** (`textures/rocks/`, `textures/nebula/proc_sheet_nebula.jpg`, `textures/tarmac/`): the OpenGameArt textures the game actually loads (terrain rocks, the sky nebula atlas and runway asphalt), copied from the full collection kept in `import/open-game-art/`. Add new OpenGameArt textures to `import/open-game-art/` and copy only the ones used into `data/textures/`.

- **Hydra and Nix** (`textures/hydra.dds`, `textures/nix.dds`): from the Orbiter 2016 textures in `import/orbiter/Textures/`, installed by `tools/celbody_import.py`.

Licensing note: the OpenGameArt textures are Creative Commons, which is fine for a free release. Before any commercial sale of Kosmos, run a full asset audit (check each licence for commercial use and attribution terms).

## Main Belt Asteroids (not shipped)

Optional Ida and Gaspra meshes come from the Orbiter Main Belt Asteroids add-on. They are not redistributed. `tools/mainbelt_import.py` converts a local copy into `data/textures-hires/`, which is git-ignored. Kleopatra and Toutatis now use the shipped NASA shapes credited in `CREDITS-models.md`.

- **Interior walls, ceilings and floors** (`textures/interior/`): the "Majestical" metal textures by Adam Foster, Randy Reddig and Robert Yang (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, <https://opengameart.org/>) for walls and ceilings, and the OpenGameArt floor plate texture and bump map for floors. Resized and repacked by `tools/bake_interior_textures.py`.

High-resolution Earth clouds (`earth_clouds.dds`, `earth_clouds/`), Earth night lights, and Mercury, Titan, Io, Europa, Enceladus, Phobos and Vesta (`<name>.dds`) are converted by `tools/orbiter_import.py` and `tools/orbiter_tiles.py` from the Orbiter 2016 high-resolution packs (`import/orbiter/`), whose imagery derives from NASA data. Credited to their authors and NASA; git-ignored and shipped only in the hi-res pack.
