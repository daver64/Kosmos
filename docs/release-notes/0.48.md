# Kosmos 0.48

- The Saturn V, MLV3 and V-23L have Payload variants with a 22 m payload section and a Payload docking port in place of the CSM. The Apollo variants retain the CSM and do not declare a payload bay. The Ares Spider gained an Aft port and stowing landing legs for carrying it as a payload; its legs start deployed.
- The separate Apollo Command Module gained Aft and Nose docking ports. KosmosMission now leaves a stack member without an attachment port out of the scenario and reports it as not placed.
- Automatic parachutes arm after the ship rises above `arm_altitude` (by default three times `auto_altitude`, at least 20 km), avoiding deployment on the pad or while stacked. Manual deployment is unchanged.
- Clouds fade near the planetary limb, and Layers and Volumetric sky clouds fade out above their tops, reducing horizon streaks.
- Scenarios can select a ship skin, and the scenario editor offers a Skin selector. The Chippenham Deltaglider scenario uses the Nuclear variant with the Shuttle_BRI skin and starts on 30 July 2020, the date of the Perseverance Mars launch.
- The Deltaglider's exterior cockpit materials are no longer overbright, and the nuclear variant has higher thermal limits.
- Aircraft control surfaces scale manual inputs with dynamic pressure; aircraft RCS can also operate in thin air at high dynamic pressure.
- OrbitComp's default sibling-planet ejection plan searches propagated planetary orbits using Lambert transfers, rather than relying solely on a circular Hohmann estimate. The search is approximate; check the predicted encounter and correct the flight as needed.
- The loading screen handles window resizing, maximizing and Alt+Enter, and the scene follows the resulting window size.
