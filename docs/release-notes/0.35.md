# Kosmos 0.35

- New Architecture Overview page on the website (linked from the Developers manual) for programmers new to the code base, and deep-dive pages on terrain, physics and rendering, the property tree and model loading, and interop and ShipEdit.
- Base `pad` accepts a `deck` height (metres above the base level) that lifts ships and flattens a small spot under the pad point; the Kennedy pad uses it so rockets stand on the mobile launcher instead of inside it. Engine plumes that hit the ground now spray as a faint, short haze rather than bright round blobs.
- New gallery screenshots (launches, orbit, Alps flights, 2D and 3D cockpits). `scripts/make-gallery.sh` regenerates them, using the airborne scenarios in `scripts/promo/`.
- Main-belt asteroids Antiope (with S2000 90 1), Kleopatra, Ida (with Dactyl), Gaspra and Toutatis join Ceres, Vesta, Pallas and Hygiea, and those four get refreshed orbits. The facts are in `data/sol.json`. The textures and shaped meshes come from Jason Miller's Orbiter Main Belt Asteroids add-on, which is not redistributed: put it in `import/orbiter/addons/MainBelt` and run `python3 tools/mainbelt_import.py` to write them into `data/textures-hires/`. Without them the bodies are plain spheres.
- Parachutes can now be drawn: a `parachutes` entry with `model` and `model_radius` shows the canopy (reefed, opening, open), and SAS is switched off while a chute is out so RCS no longer fights it. The pull now acts through the centre of mass with a damped swing toward the wind, so a capsule no longer tumbles while the chute opens. Added to the DaveMercury test addon.
- The Soyuz TMA is now three separable stages: Space drops the service module, then the orbital module, leaving the descent module to fall and open its parachute. Docking ports leave with their stage (optional `stage` key on a port).
- New capsule-only ships with drawn parachutes, descending heat shield first: CVEL Soyuz TMA Descent Module and CVEL Apollo Command Module (the Apollo three-chute cluster).
- The external camera that follows a released warhead (such as the Faslane ICBM shot) now stays on the burst for 25 seconds, at the 25 km distance it eased out to, instead of jumping back to the launching ship at detonation.
- The Sentinel ICBM's re-entry vehicle is now a slender cone with a short skirt (new `"rv"` prop) instead of the Fat Man egg; the Vulcan and field-kit bombs keep the Fat Man.
- The external camera no longer jumps forward when a stage drops: it eases to the centre of the remaining stack over a couple of seconds.
- Earth's cloud shell now fades out toward its own limb instead of ending on a hard line across the horizon when seen from high altitude. The sky shader also samples the long atmosphere chord more finely near the limb.
- Smoke, fire and the mushroom cloud are drawn as soft-edged puffs (stacked translucent shells) instead of flat discs, so the cloud no longer shows obvious circles. This is in simlib, so it needs the simlib update.
- Ships can carry their own engine sound: `"engine_sound"` in `ship.json` names a looping file in the ship folder. The Piper Cub uses its new `sounds/engine.wav`.
- The Gale Crater base model now sits on the terrain instead of floating dozens of metres above it (`y` in `data/bases/outposts.json`).
- Clouds no longer show a straight cut-off line when the view tilts toward the horizon: the near plane is pulled in and the cloud shell lifted to match.
- The Soyuz parachutes open at 500 m.
- Walking on foot beside a ship or on the ground no longer makes the ship and ground jitter sideways: the walker now follows the planet's spin in step with the physics, which runs in fixed steps.
- Plasma rifle bolts, scorch marks and blast clouds now keep in step with the planet's turn, so bolts leave the muzzle and fly straight whichever way you face, instead of starting off to one side and jittering as they recede.
- ShipEdit's 3D preview is much smoother on big models such as the Saturn V: the ship's surfaces are now drawn by OpenGL (falling back to the old software drawing if OpenGL is unavailable, or with SHIPEDIT_SOFTWARE=1), and the software path is faster too.
