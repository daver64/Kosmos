Report a problem

If Kosmos crashed or misbehaved, send the log so it can be looked at. The easiest way is the Logs page of the Kosmos launcher, which has a Report this log button and fills in the system details for you. This page is for when you cannot use the launcher.

Logs are saved by the launcher in %APPDATA%\kosmos\logs on Windows and ~/.config/kosmos/logs on Linux. You can also paste whatever Kosmos printed in its console.

Privacy: what you send is stored on the server and read only by the developer. A log can contain file paths, your graphics card and driver, and your computer name; look it over first. Nothing is sent until you press the button. Your address is kept to limit abuse.
