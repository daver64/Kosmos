# Kosmos 0.31

## New
- The launcher's Logs page has a **Report this log** button (**Report this problem** after a failed run). It shows exactly what will be sent, with home-folder paths removed, lets you leave out system details and launch options, and sends the log with an optional note and email address to the developer's server.
- A web form at `/kosmos/report/` takes a pasted or uploaded log for people who cannot use the launcher.
