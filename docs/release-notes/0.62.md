# Kosmos 0.62

## Changes

- Added damage from external overpressure and sustained environmental heat, with configurable ship ratings. F1 shows environmental readings and the HUD warns of limits and failures. The default ratings are gameplay approximations, not historical certifications.
- Added an approximate Venus atmosphere profile and made gas and ice giants surface-less: probes can descend below the model radius, with a warning where deeper synthetic values are not physical predictions. Atmospheric chemistry, corrosion and radiation are not modelled.
- Time skips are blocked during hazardous exposure or when a predicted trajectory approaches an atmosphere or enters another atmospheric body's sphere of influence; high warp slows for approaches. The conservative check may also refuse a skip it cannot verify.
- The Jupiter Luna E3 RTG scenario now starts with the probe on a Fregat in a 1,000 km orbit, ready for an entry burn and release.
- OrbitComp MAN's T+ now counts down to a fixed node time. During a main-engine burn, MAN estimates the remaining impulse against the live trajectory rather than adding the full planned burn again. The result remains an approximate instantaneous-impulse prediction; check the actual orbit and correct as needed.
- The Systems MFD shows combined solar output, including 0 W, and the F1 window lists solar sources even when they are not producing power. Removed the test-leak buttons and the F1 gas-grid display.
- Filtered blue-dominant artifacts from Earth's night-lights texture.
