# Kosmos 0.42

- The launcher shows scenario subfolders as a nested tree (for example CVEL > CVEL-Atlas), and finds a scenario's thumbnail in any thumbs/ folder above it.
- Scenario files no longer start with a number (`dragonfly_orbit.json`, not `07_dragonfly_orbit.json`), so they can be moved between folders freely. A scenario's id is still its file name, so `--scenario dragonfly_orbit` is the new form; a launcher "last scenario" saved under an old numbered id is simply not found until you pick again.
- Three new CVEL launchers imported from the Orbiter add-ons: the Proton-K (three stages, split payload fairing and a payload port; scenario at Baikonur with a Fregat upper stage carrying a Luna E3 probe), the Saturn V-23L (four liquid boosters of two F-1s each, stretched S-IC and S-IVB) and the Saturn V MLV3 (stretched S-IC, HG-3 upper stages), each with a Kennedy scenario and ascent autopilot support. The Proton's third-stage verniers are not modelled, and the V-23L booster mass is an estimate.
- Fixed the striped z-fighting on the Saturn V stages (S-IC, S-II, S-IVB, interstage): the converted meshes had a second, coincident shell inside the hull. The duplicate triangles are removed. `tools/obj_zfight.py` finds and removes such coincident triangles in any OBJ model; the same duplicate triangles are removed from the other CVEL models (Atlas, Centaur, Delta IV, Deltaglider exterior, Energia, Proton, Shuttle-A exterior, Soyuz, Transtage, Vulcan and the Saturn V parts). The cockpit models are untouched.
- Walkable interiors (habitats and other base buildings) now have textured walls, ceilings and floors: metal panel textures with normal and specular maps for the walls and ceilings, and a bolted floor plate. The rooms are a little brighter inside too. The textures are baked by `tools/bake_interior_textures.py` and credited in `data/CREDITS-textures.md`.
- New CVEL Fregat upper stage (restartable, 5.4 t of propellant, aft and payload docking ports) imported from the Orbiter add-on; the Proton-K scenario now stacks Proton, Fregat and probe.
- Time warp up to 50x is now allowed while an engine is firing in vacuum (above the atmosphere), so long burns can be sped up; it was held at 4x before. Physics substeps are finer at high warp.
- MFDs: the see-through displays are bigger, with the page text inset between the button columns so it no longer overlaps the button boxes. Every MFD has a close button (a cross at the top right; F5 brings them back), the see-through ones gain a MNU button to pick a mode, and the mode list pages (PREV/NEXT) when more than 12 modes are available.
- Walking on foot (EVA) outside a habitat or other base building with an interior no longer passes through its walls: the person stops at the wall and slides along it, and shut doors block too. (Before, being outside the building's grid counted as already blocked, so the check never applied.)
- The comms, power and ISRU buildings at the Moon, Mercury and Mars bases now have an airlock like the habitats (pressurised chamber, inner door, depressurise before going out) instead of a bare hatch.
- Base buildings with walkable interiors (habitats, quarters, power, ISRU and comms buildings) are 1.5 times larger, inside and out: wider rooms, higher ceilings, bigger domes. Their life-support volumes grow with them.
- See-through MFDs: the MNU and close buttons now sit just above the display instead of overlapping the first button box.
- The Dragonfly and Deltaglider Mars scenario now uses the nuclear Deltaglider, so the pair has power for the 308-day trip.
- Docked ships now share reserves: batteries, oxygen, nitrogen and water tanks and CO2 sorbent flow from the fuller ship to the emptier one until they are level (up to 20 kW of charge and 0.5 kg/s of consumables per link). The fuel-transfer mode "off" (`/dock/transfer`) also stops this.
- The MFD mode list uses short codes (ENG, ALT, RCS...) in the see-through displays so names fit the buttons. Addon `mfds` entries can set `"short"`.
- Ship-specific MFD modes (DG, ICBM, Shuttle-A levels) are only offered on their own ships; addons list them with `"ships"`.
- New option `--hold ID` selects a pack item at start when on foot (for example `--hold tricorder`), for screenshots and tests.
- Quantum jump is now done through portals: every system has a Quantum Portal ring in orbit of its star (about 13 AU out in Sol). Dial a star from the galaxy map or View > Quantum portal within 100 km of it, then fly through the open disc to leave from the destination's portal. The hyperjump drive requirement and the choice of arrival world are gone. New scenario "CVEL: Shuttle-A at the Sol quantum portal", ship key `portal`, scenario key `portal_m`, option `--test-dial`; `--test-jump-body` is removed.
- Invented worlds no longer stall the game on arrival: a 512-wide image is shown at once and the larger far-view images are generated in the background by two helper processes (nearest world first) and swapped in as they finish. Cached images are reused.
- New Fuel MFD: propellant by stage and RCS, burn rate and endurance, and fuel transfer buttons (AUTO, OFF, GIVE, TAKE) when docked.
- New ship CVEL Shuttle-A (Nuclear), a Shuttle-A with a 12 kW fission reactor. The Shuttle-A portal scenario now flies it as an expedition ship with two fuel pods and four life support pods docked.
- Navigation lights and strobes now start off on every ship (Y turns them on; cabin lamps still start on).
- Fixed the Shuttle-A family's 3D cockpit MFD screens being upside down; a cockpit can set `"vc_mfd_flip": true` for a model whose screen UVs are the other way up.
- The Deltaglider (and the nuclear one) now has Orbiter's three hover engines: one forward and two aft, in the same layout and thrust ratio, with the same total hover thrust as before.
- Twin levers (the Shuttle-A pod thrust pair, on the 2D panel and in the 3D cockpit) now move together when you grab between them or on their inner halves; only grabbing the outer half of one lever moves it alone.
- The Deltaglider elevator trim now works for level flight: the cockpit trim buttons (2D panel and 3D cockpit, with their directions corrected so the upper one is nose up) and new keys Ctrl+Page Up / Ctrl+Page Down trim nose up / down while held, and the trim has more authority so it can hold level flight.
- Fixed rocket ascent autopilots (Saturn V, MLV3, V-23L and the other CVEL launchers) falling out of the sky under time warp during a burn: scripts now get simulated time rather than frame time, so the pitch programme and staging timers keep pace at 10x.
- New NASA ships: Pioneer 10 and Voyager probes (from the NASA imports). Each has an aft payload port so it can ride on a launcher, real masses and hydrazine thrusters, RTGs that decay with their real half-lives, and every instrument, transmitter and computer as its own load.
- The F1 Ship systems window now has a checkbox per load, so instruments can be switched off individually to shed power as the RTGs fade; scenarios can set `systems.rtg_age_years` to start a probe part-way through its mission.
- New scenarios `NASA: Pioneer 10 inbound to Jupiter` and `NASA: Voyager inbound to Jupiter`, dated to the real flybys, with the RTGs already aged.
