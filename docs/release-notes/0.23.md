# Kosmos 0.23

- **Native addons (KosmosLink).** An addon can now include a separate program that connects to Kosmos over a local (loopback-only) socket and reads and writes properties, defines keys, spawns ships, releases ordnance, gives equipment and draws an MFD page. Nothing connects until you allow it in View > Native addons, where you also see what each addon may do and can restart its program. Options: `--link`, `--link-port N`, `--link-window`.
- **Kosmos SDK.** A separate download (`kosmos-sdk.zip` / `.tar.gz`) with the C and C++ client library, a pure-Python client, examples, an addon template, a mock server and its own manual, an extension of the modders manual.
- **Mouse look can be flipped.** Settings (F3) has "Flip left-right" and "Flip up-down" under Mouse look; they apply to the cockpit, chase, on-foot and map views and are saved.
- Scenario 14 is now *Vulcan atom bomb drop, south of Iceland*: the Vulcan starts at 8,000 ft over the North Atlantic instead of over Wagga Wagga.
- Launcher: long scenario descriptions and ship lists wrap inside their box instead of running past its edge.
- The CVEL Vulcan's landing gear now retracts and extends with G, and is hidden when stowed.
