Kosmos N1 material pass

Includes original OBJ meshes and original n1tex.png, with revised n1.mtl and a derived tangent-space normal map.

Normal map is estimated from image luminance, not baked from geometry. It may require adjusting strength or green-channel orientation in Kosmos.

PBR metallic/roughness are scalar MTL Pm/Pr values, not per-pixel maps.

All original OBJ geometry, UVs and object names remain unchanged.
