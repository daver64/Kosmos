# Kosmos 0.26

## Multiplayer weapons
- Other players' beams and sparklies are now shown in your game, with their sounds, impact flashes and lights. Shots from another player's ship are for show and do no damage.
- The multiplayer protocol changed (version 2): a 0.26 game can only be joined by 0.26 players.

## Ships
- The **CVEL Dragonfly** is rebuilt after Orbiter's: no main engine, thirteen hydrogen RCS thrusters in the left, right and aft pods (the lateral pair at 2 kN, the rest at 1 kN, Isp 408 s), each firing for the translation or rotation it serves and burning real propellant. It has a nose docking port and a cockpit panel with two MFDs, RCS mode and attitude-hold buttons.
- Ship files can now declare such thrusters: `"group": "rcs"` with `"rcs_groups"` (see the README).
- The **Deltaglider** now flies as a spaceship (RCS, SAS, the spacecraft autopilot menu) outside thick air and as an aircraft (wings, aircraft autopilot) where the atmosphere is at least a tenth of Earth's and the ship is inside it. Crossing the boundary disengages the autopilot. Ship files can opt in with `"hybrid": true`.
- The Dragonfly's docking latch and its two steerable antennas now animate (View > Animations). The Deltaglider gains a nose docking port.
- OrbitComp has a fourth page, **HEL**, showing a ship's escape trajectory in the heliocentric frame with the home planet's orbit.
- Scenarios can use `orbit.escape_to` with `orbit.escape_days` (place the ship on an escape hyperbola towards a planet, on a Lambert transfer that arrives after that many days, or a Hohmann one without it) and a top-level `set` block of property values. New scenario "CVEL: Dragonfly and Deltaglider, Mars departure" (MAVEN window, 2013-11-18), docked, with OrbitComp HEL on the left MFD. The transfer is a Lambert solution (308 days, as MAVEN's) aimed from the edge of Earth's sphere of influence, so the ship passes within about 30,000 km of Mars with no correction burn.
- Inner-planet Hohmann departures now use the retrograde transfer direction. Dated scenarios also apply in generated star systems, and an invalid `escape_to` destination is reported instead of silently starting in an ordinary orbit.

## KosmosLink
- **KosmosMap**, a new moving-map program for Linux in `addons/kosmosmap` (its source is in the SDK as a model program, `sdk/kosmosmap`), in the spirit of FlightGear's Atlas. It is a plain GTK 3 program in C++ (no simlib) that follows the flown ship over a shaded relief map of the body it is at, with the track, airports and runways, other ships, a graticule and a scale bar, and switches body when the ship leaves a sphere of influence. Tick it in **View > Native addons**. It also runs on its own as a map browser.
- A new opt-in `weapon` event tells a program when a weapon hits a ship: the ship's index, whether the shield or the hull took it, the weapon id and the ship's health.
- 3D drawing with depth on is now hidden by terrain relief and by the real extents of ships and buildings, not just bounding spheres.
- `/ships/ship[N]/health` is now published beside `shield`.
- The SDK manual describes weapons, the `fire` request and the new event; the README roadmap and the flight manual are brought up to date.

## Other
- The frame rate and frame time are published as `/sim/frame-rate` and `/sim/frame-time` and shown on the Info screen (**I**).
- Every slider has arrow buttons for single-step changes, such as the launch heading.
- Physics now runs in fixed 1/240 s steps at 4x warp and below, whatever the frame rate, so a launch behaves the same on a slow or a fast machine and a slow frame can no longer make a rocket on its pad unstable. If the machine cannot keep up, the simulation runs slower than real time instead of taking bigger steps.
- Time warp no longer stays at 4x because of buildings on other worlds, and 100000x is much lighter on the CPU.
- Fixed MFD Lua pages (notably OrbitComp's heliocentric page) going blank after a few minutes. Direct calls into Lua did not restart the script instruction budget, so it ran out cumulatively and every later draw failed.
- Mars's procedural cloud layer is much thinner (12% cover instead of 42%, and a duller tint), closer to its real patchy water-ice haze.
- Resuming your last session now restores ships exactly: their real orbit (not a circle at the same altitude), their attitude, and any docks. A docked pair used to come back as two separate ships. Saved scenario files carry an optional per-ship `state` (position, velocity, orientation) for this.
- The shipped scenarios moved from `data/scenarios/` to `scenarios/` at the top of the install. User and addon scenario folders are unchanged. The saved last session no longer lists the base buildings, which are placed from the base data anyway.
- Developer source data (Orbiter packs, NASA imports and the Celestia texture tree) now lives in `import/` instead of `data/`: `import/orbiter/`, `import/nasa/`, `import/celestia/`, `import/open-game-art/` and the raw Earth land-cover rasters in `import/landclass/` (the shipped `data/landclass/` keeps only the packed cache). If you keep your own Orbiter or Celestia packs, move them there. Nothing in `import/` is shipped.
- Distant houses and trees (drawn as billboards) no longer glow at night: they now dim with the ground's own light, so clutter is never brighter than the terrain around it. This needs the matching simlib (`ClutterLayer::set_impostor_light`).
- Buildings can have a walkable interior: an `"interior"` block in a building's `ship.json` describes square cells (floor plans as text, `#` wall, `.` floor, `D` doorway, `A` airlock hatch). Press Enter at the hatch to walk in in first person with the usual walk keys, and Enter at the hatch to go out. Walls collide from both sides. The Mare Frigoris outpost has a small two-room "quarters" building as the first example. An `"panels"` list in the interior (`pos`, `facing` of `+x`/`-x`/`+z`/`-z`, `width`) mounts the building's control panel on a wall: face it and press Enter to use it, and Enter again to step back. Sliding doors (`d` in the plan) open as you walk up to them, rooms (named by `"rooms"` seeds) are matched to the building's pressure vessels, an open door lets the air of two rooms mix, and a door stays locked while the pressure on its two sides differs by more than 5 kPa. An `"airlock"` room behind the hatch is depressurised (about 25 s) before Enter lets you out, and repressurised when you come back in. The HUD shows the room and its pressure. `W` in a plan is a window (a wall with a glazed opening you can see through but not walk through). An interior's `"lights"` list (`pos`, optional `colour`, `intensity`, `range`) are real point lights that light the room from inside and go out when the building's bus loses power. `"mesh": "file.obj"` replaces the generated walls with your own model, in interior coordinates; collision, rooms and doors still follow the plan.
