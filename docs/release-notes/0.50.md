# Kosmos 0.50

- Saved scenarios and launcher resume preserve the date, trajectories, attitude, staging, propellant and ship systems. Active autopilots and burns, debris and some transient state are not resumed; complex docking networks can lose links.
- OrbitComp accounts for destination gravity after entering a sibling body's sphere of influence, publishes the correct Lambert burn start, and estimates burn duration using main engines rather than hover thrust. Return plans identify the desired periapsis as a target; check the predicted arrival on MAN.
- Separated the quick regression suite from extended flight tests, including OrbitComp trajectory comparisons.
- Sync Orbit adds an APPR page for short-range relative motion and a bounded straight-line closest-approach estimate, with target-relative retrograde attitude hold to help match velocity before docking.
- RADALT warns of short vertical descent times and sampled terrain intersections along a constant-speed 30-second track; it is not an obstacle or stopping-distance predictor.
- Deltaglider ascent trims main-engine pitch gimbal to counter off-centre thrust.
- Experimental Mars autoland can approach nearby latitude/longitude coordinates or a base centre before landing; it does not plan an orbit-to-site descent or avoid obstacles.
- Flight recording and playback save sampled vessel states, controls and camera to a local file; playback is discrete snapshots rather than a physics re-simulation.
