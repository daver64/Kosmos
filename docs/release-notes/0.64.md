# Kosmos 0.64

## Changes

- Added **File > Set date and time** to rebuild the environment at a UTC date. The flight is restored and paused; landed vessels stay at their surface locations, while orbiting vessels retain body-relative states. The dialog explains the state that is reset or lost and the situations that must be stopped first.
- Added approximate walkable compartments to Salyut, Lunar Salyut, ISS, Mir, Wheel and Gateway stations. Use **View > Explore station interior** to float inside; Enter returns to the controls.
- Added the fictional CVEL Shuttle 2010 and a Kennedy launch scenario. Its separate launch stack supplies ascent thrust; undock and switch to the shuttle after cutoff. Flight characteristics and guidance are approximate.
- Atlantis now carries Centaur G, Fregat and Luna E3 in its bay, with an orbital deployment scenario and animated bay doors. Its thrust alignment, thrust and propellant have been adjusted. RCS remains functional but invisible on Atlantis, Buran and Shuttle 2010.
- Ariane 44L at Kourou now carries Fregat and Luna E3. Adjusted its loaded ascent guidance and fixed overlapping third-stage nose geometry.
- ShipEdit's **New blank** starts with a fuelled rocket and ascent autopilot. Imported stage animation controls are retained, and launch-readiness problems are reported. Added an N1 Soyuz stack built with ShipEdit.
- Added initial macOS build support; it is experimental and the GTK tool windows have known limitations. See the draft macOS build guide.
