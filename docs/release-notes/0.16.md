## Kosmos 0.16

### Ships
- New addon `cvel-saturn-v`: the CVEL Saturn V imported from the Orbiter add-on, with its meshes and the masses, thrust and Isp from its source. It has four stages (S-IC, S-II, S-IVB and the command and service module) and a three-man cabin. New scenario *Apollo 11: CVEL Saturn V, T-5 minutes* puts it on Pad 39A at Kennedy five minutes before the 1969 launch.
- More CVEL addons, all under a new *CVEL* scenario submenu: `cvel-atlas` (Atlas III and Centaur, on the Kennedy pad), `cvel-centaur` (Centaur G, Kennedy), `cvel-delta-iv` (Delta IV Heavy, Kennedy), `cvel-vulcan` (Avro Vulcan on the Twickenham runway) and `cvel-transtage` (circular 1500 km orbit).
- `cvel-deltaglider` (Chippenham runway), `cvel-shuttle-a` (Helsinki pad) and `cvel-dragonfly` (1500 km orbit) use the meshes and textures from the open-source Orbiter repository (MIT) with their Orbiter masses and thrust.
- Ships can set `"horizontal": true` to rest level on their gear rather than nose-up, as the Shuttle-A does.
- Engine plumes can be tuned with `plume_width`, `plume_glow` and `plume_density`, and a ship can set `camera_target` so the external camera looks at a chosen point (for example near the capsule on a tall rocket). User orbit cameras honour it too.
- Engines can have a different Isp at sea level and in a vacuum. Give an engine `isp` (vacuum) and `isp_sl` and its thrust and Isp change with air pressure as in Orbiter, while propellant flow stays constant.

### Planets
- Hi-res Earth tiles now blend in softly: a patch of finer tiles fades in as it loads, and where it meets coarser ground its edge feathers instead of showing a hard line.

- Engines can be put in a `hover` group with its own throttle (`/controls/hover`, Keypad + − 0 .), used by the Shuttle-A, which also gets its Orbiter 2D panel.
- Tools > Start/Stop recording video saves the screen and the game's sound to an H.264/AAC `.mp4` through ffmpeg; `--record SECS` records a scripted clip.
- Exhaust no longer passes through the ground: within 150 m of the surface the plume particles are turned and sprayed outward along it.

## Deltaglider (CVEL)

- Engines, masses and touchdown points now follow the CVEL source: two 120 kN scramjet mains (Isp 4077 s), a 270 kN hydrogen hover engine, 12 t dry mass and 11.4 t propellant. A scramjet with an explicit `"air_breathing": false` keeps the scramjet plume but performs like a rocket.
- The 2D panel is the CVEL one, with working AOA and slip dials and the horizon. The retro engines are not ported.
- New ship properties `rate-pitch`, `rate-yaw` and `rate-roll` (deg/s).
- The panel also has slope, propellant and engine-level bars. The overhead rate gauges are not wired.
- Aerodynamics are retuned: the Deltaglider and Vulcan have delta-wing lift and induced drag (`cl_alpha` 2.6, stall at 30 degrees), and the brick-shaped Shuttle-A has almost no lift but a lot of drag. Lift and drag scale with air density, so none of it acts in vacuum.
- Hover: with the hover engines firing, the aircraft-class ships hold themselves level with RCS (pitch and roll levelled, yaw damped), so an off-centre hover thrust no longer flips the ship. RCS thrust is raised.
- Keypad + − 0 . 1-9 now work as keys (simlib had no keypad scancodes), so the hover throttle can be driven from the keypad.

## Camera and flight fixes
- Dragging in the chase view now orbits the ship, keeping it centred, instead of turning the view in place (which lost the ship after staging).
- The chase camera can no longer drop below the terrain, and a crashed ship is lifted clear of the ground rather than sinking into it.

## Windows and graphics backends
- Vulkan now prefers a discrete GPU over an integrated one on a machine with both (`SIMLIB_VULKAN_DEVICE_TYPE=discrete|integrated|any` overrides it). The OpenGL renderer and the Vulkan device are printed at startup.
- Resizing or maximising the window under Direct3D 12 could freeze the game; the swap chain resize was fixed to refresh its back buffer index and to recover if the resize fails. **This is untested on Windows.**
- Fixed: the Windows build had no JPEG decoder, so every `.jpg` texture (planets, Earth lights and so on) failed to load. It is now linked with libjpeg-turbo.
- Known issues, reported on Windows with an NVIDIA GPU and not reproduced on Linux: Vulkan can show heavy graphical corruption (blocky black patches on the ground and sky), and OpenGL runs very slowly (about 5 fps) though it renders correctly. Resizing works under Vulkan. If you see either, try `--d3d12`, check that Kosmos is using your NVIDIA GPU (Windows Settings > Graphics > High performance), and report the `OpenGL renderer:` / `Vulkan device:` lines printed at startup.
