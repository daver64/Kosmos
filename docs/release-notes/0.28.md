# Kosmos 0.28

## Tools
- The Windows game package now ships KosmosMap and the planetbake window (GTK 3, with its schemas), built by the new `scripts/build-win-tools.sh`, which `build-windows.sh` runs first.
- planetbake, a new program in `addons/planetbake/bin` next to KosmosMap (also in the SDK, with its source), bakes a generated world into colour, height, normal, specular, night-lights and cloud maps as PNG or DDS (BC1, BC3 or uncompressed, with mip chains), as single images up to 16384 wide or as resumable tile pyramids up to 65536 wide. It has a GTK 3 window with a 1024x512 preview and a button for each layer, and a command line. It uses the game's own generator, so the maps match the game, and never more than 4 threads. The SDK download carries its source and Linux and Windows programs.
- `--gen-world` and `--bake-planet` print the planetbake command line that makes the same world.
- `--gen-world NAME` (with `--system`) makes one world's far-view image into the cache from the command line, up to 16384 wide, on at most 4 threads and 4 GB (`--gen-width`, `--gen-threads`, `--gen-memory`). Settings > World texture 16384 still makes every world in a system.
- KosmosMap's scan is capped at 4 threads, and the build and media scripts (make-gallery, make-promo, the build and release scripts) never use more than 4.
- planetbake can open an existing pack (`--open DIR`, or the window's Open pack button) and show it layer by layer. Click a tile to edit it in your graphics program (Krita unless you set another with `--set-editor`); the saved picture is taken back into the pack and locked. `--fill` fills missing tiles, levels and layers from a picture or folder (for example a Celestia or Orbiter texture folder), from a downloaded NASA dataset (`--list-sources`), from the pack's coarser levels, or from the generator. `.locked` markers (`--lock`, `--unlock`) protect a tile, layer or pack from baking and filling. Sources must be equirectangular; the Windows program has no window.

- KosmosAV (Kosmos Air Virtual), a shared freight and passenger network, has started: its server `kosmosavd` (sign-up requests, members' message board with screenshots, roster and who-is-flying-where pages, and a job board for the Deltaglider, Shuttle-A and Dragonfly with credits and a ledger, paid by distance, delta-v and mass) and a desktop program, `KosmosAV`, which is a native addon (View > Native addons) that reads your ship and base through KosmosLink, takes and gives back jobs and completes them when you land at the destination. Ships now publish `/ships/ship[N]/class` over the link. The desktop program is built for Linux (libcurl) and Windows (WinHTTP). Deliveries cannot be faster than 20 s plus 8 s per km/s of delta-v. Interstellar jobs run between Sol and freight depots at Sirius Prime, Proxima b, Thalassa (Tau Ceti) and TRAPPIST-1 e, paid by the light year, and only the new Shuttle-AQ can take them. See `kav/docs/DESIGN.md`.
## Ships and scenarios
- New CVEL Shuttle-AQ, a Shuttle-A with a 50 light year quantum jump drive (it shares the Shuttle-A's model and panels), the interstellar freighter of KosmosAV; four freight depot bases (`data/bases/depots.json`) at Sirius Prime, Proxima b, Thalassa and TRAPPIST-1 e; and scenario 17, the Shuttle-AQ on the Thalassa depot pad.
- New CVEL Piper Cub addon (a taildragger, textured model from the Blender file) and scenario 16, parked on the runway at Chippenham. Its propeller does not spin yet, and it needs rudder on the take-off run.
- The CVEL Soyuz TMA animates, ported from the Orbiter addon: View > Animations > Panels and antennae unfolds both solar panels, the antennae and the nose covers over 10 seconds.

## Fixes
- The CVEL Delta IV Heavy now lifts off: its centre core engine was only in the second stage, so the two boosters alone could not lift 711 t. It now burns with the boosters.
- Launcher thumbnails: scenarios 06 to 13 and 15 had missing or stale preview images (left over from an earlier numbering); they are regenerated, and the Soyuz, outpost-base and target-sphere addons have thumbnails.
- A joystick axis that first reads fully pinned (a stick that has not reported its rest position) is ignored until it moves, instead of holding the controls hard over.

## Documentation
- KosmosMap has a player guide (the manual's new KosmosMap section and the README) and developer notes on how it is built (`sdk/kosmosmap/README.md`, the SDK manual's worked example).
- `scripts/make-gallery.sh` takes the gallery screenshots with Kosmos' own `--shot`.
- A body can name a planetbake pack with `"tiles": "NAME"` (a folder of `colour`, `specular`, `normal` tile pyramids under `data/textures-hires/`), and the home planet then draws it as its tiled imagery. A baked cloud map (`clouds.dds` or `NAME_clouds.dds/png` in the pack folder, or `<body>_clouds` in `data/textures-hires/`) is used for the cloud shell when present; otherwise the procedural clouds are used as before. New scenario `CVEL/15_argo_thalassa` puts the Argo in a 1500 km orbit of Thalassa, an Earth-like world in the Tau Ceti system, using such a pack.
- planetbake adds fine land colour detail at high resolution (`--detail`, default 1), since the generator's own colour is smooth below about 100 km.
- The Argo's navigation lights moved to its wing tips, and its strobe and headlight were removed (it has only the red and green nav lights).
- Nebulae are now thin, wispy, large-scale filaments generated for each system (replacing the lumpy sprite sheet), drawn unlit so they show from the dark side of a planet too. The sky's dust lanes and blob nebulae are softened.
