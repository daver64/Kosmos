# Kosmos 0.22

- Changed: on foot, Space now jumps and Ctrl crouches (they were the other way round); in zero gravity Space moves you up and Ctrl down.
- New: on foot, every building's door is marked on screen ("DOOR <name> <distance>") up to 300 m away, and "Press Enter to go into <name>" appears when you are close enough. The door reach is a little more generous.
- Fixed: jumping while pressed against a building (or any solid) threw you away at over 100 m/s, because the planet's spin was being cancelled along with your own motion.
- Changed: nights on Mars and other thin-air worlds are darker; the surface fill light after dusk is much lower.
- Fixed: the base object types `solar_array` and `solar_windmill` stood on edge with half the panel under the ground (and the windmill looked like a satellite). Both are now tilted ground racks on posts, as at Gale Crater.
- Changed: the Gale building panels are laid out in three columns (status, controls, power bus) with section headings. Gauges now show their reading inside the bar, and switches carry their power lamp inside the button. For modders, a `label` control with a width draws a rule under its text.
- New: on foot, the ` (backquote) key switches between mouselook and a free pointer for the menus, for desktops where Alt-click is taken by the window manager.
- Fixed: the suit HUD altitude and vertical speed flickered between values when standing on the ground. Within 0.2 m of the ground the altitude now reads 0.0.
- New: the Tranquility Base (Moon), Mare Frigoris outpost (Moon) and Caloris outpost (Mercury) now have working buildings you can enter, like Gale Crater's: a habitat, a power plant and a comms mast at each, and an ISRU plant at Tranquility. They come from a new `outpost-base` addon.
- New: building interfaces. A building's panel can now be viewed as a 3D board (press C inside a building: chase, 2D panel, then 3D) with clickable controls, and can use a background bitmap via `layout.image`. The Gale and outpost habitats have one. For modders, a building may name its block `"interface"` instead of `"cockpit"`; `layout.position`, `size` and `tilt` place the 3D board.
- New: the Shuttle-A's two aux engine pods now work as in Orbiter. They sit amidships, start thrusting retrograde and swing at 0.2 rad/s through hover (straight down) to forward, with the pod model, plume and thrust direction following. Control them from the panel (aux pod sliders, tilt switches and FWD/HOVER/RETRO presets) or with Keypad 9/3 (thrust), 8/2 (swing) and 4/5/6 (presets). The panel's RCS mode switch (LIN/ROT/OFF) works too. For modders: engine group `pod`, ship key `rcs_modes`, and animation properties `pod-left` and `pod-right`.
- Changed: the weather line at the bottom of the screen now appears for a few seconds when the METAR or station changes, instead of staying up all the time.
- 3D cockpit instrument boards are now sampled with bilinear filtering, which stops thin MFD lines (such as the Surface MFD pitch ladder) shimmering.
- New: the Deltaglider's nose cone opens and closes (View > Animations > Nose hatch, or the NOSE HATCH button on the overhead panel), swinging its four petals open to reveal the docking port. The Deltaglider's panel RCS mode knob (OFF/ROT/LIN) now works too.
- New: ships can list named animations the player can trigger. Add an `animation_controls` list to `ship.json` (`name`, `property`, `duration`) and drive a model animation from that property. They appear under View > Animations and as `/controls/anim/<property>`.
- The Deltaglider glides better: its constant parasitic drag is cut from 4 to 1.5 m², raising best lift-to-drag from about 4.9 to about 6.5.
- New: equipment and ordnance. On foot you carry a pack of equipment (keys 1 to 9 pick an item, left mouse uses it): a tricorder with a live readout, a geo scanner and a survey theodolite (click to mark points and measure between them), a pistol, a plasma rifle and thrown demolition charges, shown as simple stand-in shapes in an FPS view. Weapons hurt ships and people; your suit has health, and you revive aboard your ship if you are killed. Ships and buildings can list a `locker` of extra items you take when you leave them (the Deltaglider, Shuttle-A and the base habitats carry a plasma rifle and charges).
- New: ships can carry droppable ordnance on `hardpoints`. The CVEL Vulcan carries 21 1000 lb bombs and a Fat Man atom bomb; K drops the next bomb and Shift+K the special store. Bombs fall under gravity and drag, take the aircraft's mass with them, and leave a scorch mark. The atom bomb airbursts at 500 m with a blinding flash, a shock wave that damages ships and people, a mushroom cloud that turns muddy brown and stands for six minutes, and a deep rumble (`data/sounds/nuke.mp3`). Scenario 14, *Vulcan bombing run, Wagga Wagga*, starts you over the target.
- New: the `field-kit` addon defines the stock equipment. For modders, equipment is JSON in `<addon>/equipment/*.json`, ships take `hardpoints` and `locker` in `ship.json`, and simlib gains `make_bomb_data`, `make_fatman_data` and a 3D particle library (`particles3d.h`) with muzzle flash, smoke, explosion, plasma and mushroom effects.
- New: ship lights can be switched. Y toggles navigation lights and strobes, Shift+Y the landing and nose lights, and both are in the View menu. The properties are `/controls/lighting/nav` and `/controls/lighting/lamps`.
- Fixed: the Vulcan's wingtip lights now sit on its wingtips.
- New: the keyboard is rebindable. View > Keyboard lists every key action in two slots; click to rebind, right-click to clear, Reset to restore. Shift chords and modifier keys for held controls work, flying and walking can share keys, and the choices are saved in `keys.json`. Menu hints follow your bindings.
- New: addons can add bindable key actions with a `keys.json` (toggle or hold a property).
