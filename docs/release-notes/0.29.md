# Kosmos 0.29

## Terrain
- A base can set a mesh into the ground: `terrain_meshes` in a base file takes an `.stl`, `.obj`, `.glb` or `.gltf`, places it in the base frame (`x`, `z`, `heading`, `scale`, `vscale`, `y`) and its surface becomes the terrain, for ships, wheels and walkers alike. The base is levelled into it and clutter keeps off it. It is a height map, so overhangs and caves are not solid.
- The mesh's own textures or vertex colours colour the ground where it has them; `"texture": "terrain"` keeps the terrain's colouring instead, and `image` drapes a picture over an STL, which has no texture of its own.
- Gale Crater base now sits in the scanned crater from `import/nasa/Gale Crater.stl`; new scenario CVEL 19, Rover, Gale Crater.

## Sky
- Low over another world that has air (Mars, Venus, Titan, and any authored world with an atmosphere) the sky and haze now take the colour of that atmosphere, instead of a black sky with stars. Mars is butterscotch by day. The home world's sky is unchanged.

## Vehicles
- Wheeled vehicles now stop at buildings, other ships, trees and rocks the way a person on foot does, and lose the speed they had into the obstacle. Terrain was already solid under the wheels.
- The chase camera on a staged rocket follows the stages still attached, so it stays on the Soyuz capsule after the last stage leaves.
