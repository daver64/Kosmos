# Kosmos 0.19

- Fixed bright grey rectangles on Earth's night side from orbit: the optional hi-res `earth_lights.dds` had flat grey blocks where it should be black, which the additive city-lights layer drew. If you have your own copy of that file, replace it or delete it to fall back to `earth_lights.jpg`.
- Fixed (Windows): the Windows build had no JPEG decoder, so every `.jpg` texture (planets, Earth lights and so on) failed to load. It is now linked with libjpeg-turbo.
- Tools > Screenshot waits a moment so the menu has closed before it captures.
- New: Kosmos Launcher (`kosmos-launcher`), a separate program for choosing a scenario with a preview, enabling or disabling addons, installing addons from a zip, managing the hi-res texture pack, setting launch options, checking for and downloading new versions, and reading crash logs. Addons may add `description`, `author`, `thumbnail` and `long_description` to `addon.json`.
- Quitting Kosmos saves the session (ships, positions, the ship you were flying, and the date) as the scenario "Last session" in your scenarios folder, and the launcher's "Resume last scenario" button loads it.
- Fixed switching to a scenario with a different date from a base or pad: the ship was placed at the wrong height, fell through the ground and was thrown off or destroyed.
- New: graphics settings (F3 and the launcher's Graphics page): render style (normal, flat shaded, wireframe, for slow or software graphics such as VNC), clouds, maximum frame rate, base light radius and terrain detail. `--render-style` sets the style from the command line. Works on the OpenGL, Vulkan and Direct3D 12 backends.
- New: joystick support (View > Joystick). Separate aircraft and spacecraft profiles with per-action axis and button binding by "press to bind", invert, dead zone and curve, saved in `joystick.json`.
- Fixed: Vulkan 3D cockpit instruments were upside down. The Vulcan now has the Skylark's 2D and 3D cockpits, its gear sits on the ground, and it can take off. Flat shaded and wireframe render styles were fixed on Vulkan (missing objects, white or black planets).
