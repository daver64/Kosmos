## Kosmos 0.17

### Stars and the quantum drive
- New sky: a smooth Milky Way with faint nebulae replaces the blocky background, and the brightest spots are dimmer.
- Stars are drawn as glowing, swirling suns with limb darkening, a corona, glare and rays, including our own Sun. Stars no longer flicker or z-fight against the scene.
- Stars without a hand-built system now get procedurally generated planets.
- Ships with a `hyperjump` engine (the Argo) have a quantum jump drive (`range_ly`, 50 light years by default). Open **View > Quantum navigation**, choose a star in range and engage. The galaxy map's Enter key jumps too.
- You arrive in orbit of the best world in the target system: an Earth-like planet if there is one, otherwise a Mars- or Venus-like rocky planet, any rocky planet, a moon of a gas giant, or the giant itself. `--orbit-body auto` does the same from the command line.
- Jumps happen inside the running program. The window stays open while a short hyperspace effect plays and the new system loads, instead of closing and reopening.

### Ships
- The Skylark no longer has an afterburner plume.

### Diagnostics
- When a surface texture cannot be loaded, the message now includes the loader's reason.
- New `--test-jump N` option jumps to star N a second into the run (for testing).
