# Kosmos 0.37

- New orbital stations from the Orbiter meshes: the ISS, Mir and the Wheel, each with a month of life support for two people. A "Crew arrive / Crew depart" button in the systems window switches the station's systems and life support on and off. The new CVEL "Orbital stations" scenario places dormant ISS stations around Earth, Jupiter and Saturn, and Mir stations around every planet, Earth's Moon, Europa and Ganymede, with a Wheel as well.
- New Shuttle-A Life Support Pod for the spine bays: docked, it tops up the shuttle's oxygen, nitrogen, water and CO2 sorbent from a further year's supply for two. Use as many as you like (`"life_pod": true` in a ship file marks any supply pod).
- Dragonfly: carries a week of life support for one crew member (the cabin crew is now 1).
- The Deltaglider and the Shuttle-A can now make their own supplies on the ground: an ice miner (water, scaled by how wet the body is, plus nitrogen on bodies with air and CO2 sorbent regeneration), an electrolyser (oxygen) and a propellant refinery. Start it from the Ship systems window or `/ships/ship[N]/systems/processing`. New component `refinery` and `"site"`, `n2_kg_per_day`, `regen_kg_per_day` on `isru`.
- Shuttle-A: carries life support for a crew of two for a year (oxygen, nitrogen and water tanks, a pressure regulator and a CO2 scrubber). The RCS modes work again when manoeuvring, and its RCS thrust (40 kN) and RCS propellant (6000 kg) are raised.
- New Sync Orbit MFD: range, closing speed, phase and period ratio to a target, with a phasing or Hohmann plan (ORB+ / ORB- change the phasing orbit count). It only says "Phased with target" when you really are within about 30 m.
- Transfer MFD: shows dV left on the burn, and a new AUTO button flies burn 1 for you (holds prograde, slows time warp, ignites centred on the window and stops when the apoapsis reaches the target's orbit; ABORT cancels). Expect to trim the approach yourself. The diagram now shows the central body, your actual orbit (an ellipse once you are on the transfer), the target's orbit, the planned transfer ellipse and a dot for you and the target; Sync Orbit's diagram shows your real orbit and labels both dots. OrbitComp's PLN page draws the same diagram, with the Sun at the centre for a planet-to-planet plan.
- Deltaglider: the main throttle goes into reverse (the red RET zone on the throttle lever, with the panel and cockpit sliders reversing in 2D and 3D); zero thrust sits at the top of that zone. Ships opt in with `"reverse_throttle": true`.
- Deltaglider: now has real life support for two crew (oxygen, nitrogen and water tanks, a pressure regulator and a CO2 scrubber, enough oxygen, water and CO2 sorbent for one person for six months), so the CO2 alarm no longer sounds a few hours into a flight.
- Deltaglider: the right-hand buttons on both panel MFDs work, and the HUD now has orbit, surface and docking modes instead of one.
- Deltaglider virtual cockpit has a new clean brushed-aluminium, modern-airliner look.
- Pressing H shows the HUD mode as an on-screen message, changing the field of view shows the new angle, and the info line shows both.
- Home resets the cockpit view to forwards, as in Orbiter. Hover full / off moved to Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End.
- New ship: CVEL Deltaglider (Nuclear), a Deltaglider with an 8 kW fission reactor (+1200 kg dry mass) so ice mining and the refinery do not depend on sunlight.
- New scenarios: CVEL: Nuclear Deltaglider, Gale Crater; CVEL: Shuttle-A with life support pods, orbit; CVEL: Shuttle-A ice mining, Tranquility Base.
- New tool: KosmosCAD (Linux and Windows), a 3D modeller with four OpenGL views, mesh and sub-object editing, shape creation, booleans and equipment markers; ShipEdit has a "Model in KosmosCAD" button.
- KosmosCAD: File > Close file (Ctrl+W) clears the editor, and opening a JSON file that is not a ship.json shows a warning instead of doing nothing. A manual and tutorial are on the web site.
