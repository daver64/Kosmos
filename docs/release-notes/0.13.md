## Kosmos 0.13

### Fixes
- Earth was turned 180° when seen from space: Wagga Wagga appeared over Africa and the continents were mirrored. Orbit and high-altitude views now match the terrain below.
- The default start time is now your real local time (the Earth's day/night follows it). A scene's JSON can still set its own date, and `--date` overrides it.
- Right-drag and mouse movement now turn the camera instead of moving the planet.
- The start date is no longer printed on screen; it is in the info text.

### Ships and effects
- Hydrogen engines in vacuum now give a short, tight plume with shock diamonds, like the ramjet, instead of a billowy one. Sea-level plumes are unchanged.
- The Ares now uses hydrogen engines (Isp 440), so its Mars performance differs from before.
- Nuclear engines (the Argo) have no plume: a soft neon blue-white glowing panel shows at the nozzle.

### Interface
- The menu bar is pinned open by default, with more padding on the left of the text. Untick *View → Keep menu bar visible* to hide it.
- New *View → 2D Panels* submenu lists the instrument panels a ship defines and shows the chosen one in the 2D cockpit.
- The Skylark has two panels: *Flight instruments* (the six-pack plus engine and surface MFDs) and *Navigation*.

### For addon authors
- `cockpit.layout.name` and a `cockpit.panels` list define extra 2D panels; see the modding guide.
- New command-line options: `--panel N`, `--shot-stages N`.

### Known issues
- Some users have reported clipping at the cloud layer; this has not been reproduced yet.
