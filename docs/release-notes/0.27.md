# Kosmos 0.27

## Ships and weapons
- The Deltaglider, Dragonfly and Shuttle-A carry navigation lights (red port, green starboard, white tail; `/controls/lighting/nav`); the Vulcan's are repositioned. Distant lights are smaller and dimmer so they no longer swamp the ship.
- Beams are thinner at a distance and beam weapons use the new `Rifleprimary2.ogg` sound.
- A hit on a ship now gives a single small flare: no sparks, bounce or explosion. Ground hits are unchanged.
- Ships may set `"hit_sphere": true` in `ship.json` so weapons hit a ball of the stage radius instead of the bounding box. The new "Target sphere" addon ship, a shielded ball for weapons practice, uses it.
- The Deltaglider's gear and wheel covers animate the right way round when it starts airborne. Sparkle bolts are slimmer, and their trails no longer draw hard green streaks in the distance or start behind the muzzle when the camera is far back.
- The Saturn V's engines use a shorter, softer plume: engines may set `"plume_billow"` (0 to 1) to keep the shock diamonds with a billowing exhaust. The old Saturn V at Kennedy is gone; use the CVEL Saturn V.

## Rendering
- Clip planes now count ships docked or flying within 300 m of the camera, so a docked partner no longer clips when you fly the other ship.

## Scenarios
- New scenario "CVEL: Mare quarters, on foot" starts you inside the walkable quarters. `--enter-building` now walks you in whenever the building has an interior.

## Command line (for video and screenshots)
- New options `--scenario-file`, `--set-at`, `--launch-at`, `--drop`, `--alone`, `--cam-tilt`, `--cam-dolly`, `--auto-stage`, `--record-after-stage`, `--clean`, `--interior-at`, `--sun-angle`. `--sunlit` now works with `--orbit`, and `--orbit-alt` is honoured for orbit starts and for the circular orbits of a scenario. See the README.
- `scripts/make-promo.sh` builds a showreel from real footage recorded with `--record`.
- A recorded run that forces sound on no longer overwrites your saved settings.
