# Star Textures by Spectral Type

Place star surface textures here organized by spectral class:

## Spectral Classes
- `O_class.png` - Blue supergiants (30,000-50,000K)
- `B_class.png` - Blue-white (10,000-30,000K)
- `A_class.png` - White (7,500-10,000K)
- `F_class.png` - Yellow-white (6,000-7,500K)
- `G_class.png` - Yellow like our Sun (5,200-6,000K)
- `K_class.png` - Orange (3,700-5,200K)
- `M_class.png` - Red dwarfs (2,400-3,700K)

## Our Sun
- `Sun_color.png` (G2V class) - 8K recommended
- `Sun_emissive.png` - Corona/atmosphere effects

Stars can also be rendered procedurally with appropriate color temperature.
Resolution: 1K-2K typical, procedural generation as fallback.
