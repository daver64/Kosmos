# Kosmos 0.18

- Deltaglider: animated landing gear (ported from Orbiter), stronger main and hover engines.
- **G** now toggles the landing gear; the galaxy map moved to **N**.
- Cockpit view: arrow keys and PgUp/PgDn move your head.
- Invented planets and moons are no longer plain balls: every generated world is Mercury-, Venus-, Earth-, Mars-like or a gas giant, with its own surface image (made on first visit, cached in `~/.cache/kosmos/planets`) and real streaming terrain on rocky ones.
- `--bake-planet` pre-generates a high-resolution tile set for a generated world (see the README for disk use); `--gen-planet` previews a world type.
- Scenarios can name a star system (`"system"`), and there is an Argo orbit scenario at each real star within quantum range of Sol.
- `--bake-lazy` makes high-resolution tiles on demand as you fly, instead of all up front.
- Quantum navigation: pick a particular planet or moon to arrive at, or leave it on "Best world" for the old habitable-first choice.
- Fixed a crash after a quantum jump (the flown ship was read after the jump had deleted it).
- `--list-worlds mercury|venus|earth|mars|gas` lists invented worlds of that kind within quantum range, nearest first.
- The night side of planets and moons is now properly dark (ships keep their fill light).
- New scenario: Argo in geosynchronous orbit over a half-lit Earth, ready for a quantum jump.
- Invented Earth-like, Venus-like and Mars-like worlds now have procedural clouds (cream and thick on Venus-like, thin and pinkish on Mars-like).
- Choosing another scenario no longer reloads the whole system: same-system scenarios keep the terrain, textures and clouds, even when they start on a different date (the planets and the terrain turn are simply moved to the new date). `--test-scenario ID` switches scenario mid-run for testing.
- Settings (F3) has a "World texture" choice (Automatic, 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384) for the far-view image of invented worlds, and `--world-texture WIDTH` sets it from the command line. The change applies at once. Each size is generated once and cached (about 35 MB at 4096, 135 MB at 8192, 540 MB at 16384 per world; see the Modders manual before choosing large sizes for many worlds).
