# Kosmos 0.39

- Re-entry plasma starts from the part of the hull that faces the airstream (a bounding ellipsoid of the vessel) and is shed across its whole front, so a belly-first Deltaglider glows across its underside instead of only at the nose.
- The Map MFD's target base name, range and bearing are right-aligned so a long name no longer runs off the display.
- HUD speed and altitude readouts: the green frames now stand clear of the text and draw whole instead of being cut by the text backing.
- Time warp: above the atmosphere you can now warp up to 1000x even when the orbit dips back into the air. The warp steps down as the air approaches and is at 4x by the top of the atmosphere.
- Cabin ambience: inside a cockpit, interior or pressurised room, one of the XRSound cabin clips plays at random every 2 to 6 minutes.
- See-through (HUD) MFDs: the button labels are now clickable. Each labelled button has a green outline that flashes red when pressed.
- Deltaglider and other hybrids: in the atmosphere the pitch, yaw and roll keys drive the control surfaces even with the RCS switch off or in linear mode; the switch only silences the thrusters.
- The camera that watches a warhead burst (ICBM shot) now turns with the Earth, so the mushroom cloud no longer drifts sideways across the frame.
- Time acceleration above 4x is smoother: the simulation now advances by a smoothed frame time, so millisecond timer rounding and the odd slow frame no longer show up as jerky motion at 1000x and above.
- Re-entry plasma is redrawn as a stream of glowing puffs shed from the nose, like rocket exhaust: a bright white-yellow head and a soft wake fading through orange to red, instead of stacked translucent shells.
- Hybrid ships (Deltaglider, Shuttle-A and the like) now get RCS attitude and translation once the air is thin (under 500 Pa dynamic pressure) instead of only above the whole atmosphere. The cockpit RCS switch in OFF also stops SAS, no hold button stays lit, and pressing a lit hold button releases it.
- Deltaglider ascent: flying nose-first at Mach 5 in thick air overheated the airframe (its bare-skin limit was 650 K), which silently killed the main engines and RCS even with the throttle at 100% and fuel left. The limit is now 1200 K, and the hypersonic trim moment no longer fights the pilot while the engines are firing.
- Deltaglider re-entry: the hypersonic pitching moment is now limited like the capsules', so it holds a steady nose-high attitude (about 40° angle of attack) through the heating instead of flipping between 5° and 75°.
- Capsule re-entry (Soyuz, Apollo): the plasma is a much longer, brighter yellow streak at peak heating; the aerodynamic moment is limited to a stable oscillation so the capsule no longer spins up in dense air, and it keeps its heat shield forward all the way down to the parachutes; attitude RCS and SAS stand down while the air holds the capsule; and a capsule now lands on its parachutes (it used to sink through the ground and be reported lost), only a touchdown faster than 15 m/s wrecks it.
- KosmosCAD keeps textures with the model: image paths in a `.kmodel` or `ship.json` are now relative to its folder, and an image picked from outside that folder is copied into a `textures` folder beside it (older absolute paths are fixed on the next save).
