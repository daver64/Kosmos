# Kosmos 0.33

## Changes
- New **Edit > Performance settings** window in the sim, next to Settings. It gathers the launcher's graphics options (render style, clouds, weather, terrain detail, frame-rate limit, light radius, Earth relief), the scenery (trees, buildings and rocks) and its density, and the textures, including a new saved "Largest texture" setting. These moved out of the F3 Settings window.
- New saved scenery draw-distance setting (25 to 200 %) next to the scatter density, to trade distance for frame rate.
- New **Cloud style** option in Performance settings: Simple (the single deck, still the default), Layers (every reported METAR layer, up to four, in the sky) or Volumetric (ray-marched 3D towers on cumulus and storm cloud; heavy on the GPU). Layers and Volumetric also grey the view while you fly inside thick cloud.
- Fixed thunder and lightning continuing at 30 km and above. Storms now stop a few km above the highest cloud top.
- Fixed clouds being sliced off along a line across the view as you climb: the near plane (and the Earth cloud shell) now leave room for off-axis cloud.
- Lightning now draws textured bolts from `data/textures/lightning/` (up to three at once) and thunder plays the recording `data/sounds/lightning.wav`. Thunder is rarer (a strike every 12 to 42 seconds at full storm) and its volume and pitch follow the strike's distance: near strikes crack loud, far ones rumble quietly. A faint far rumble is skipped while a louder one is still rolling.
- New **re-entry model**: ships with a `thermal` block heat up by Sutton-Graves stagnation heating, radiate, and can carry an ablative shield; a `hypersonic` block blends in a modified-Newtonian aero model with a trim moment above a set speed; a `parachutes` block adds reefed, opening and open canopies with auto-deploy and a max-q tear-away. Delete deploys and Shift+Delete cuts the parachutes (`/controls/parachute`). Telemetry is at `/ships/ship[N]/thermal/*` and `/chutes/*`. The Deltaglider and Soyuz TMA have the blocks.
- Falling ordnance now heats up and can burn up; hardpoints take `eject` and `eject_speed`. Yield, burst mode and burst height can be set per release with `/controls/ordnance/{yield-kt,mode,burst-height}`, and blast, mushroom and thermal radius scale with the cube root of yield.
- New **CVEL Sentinel ICBM** addon (two stages, split fairing, Fat Man warhead, ICBM Targeting MFD for lat/lon, air or ground burst and 20 kt to 1 Mt) and the Faslane base with scenario 21. The same targeting settings are in the **View > ICBM targeting** window (range 300 to 4900 km).
- Cockpit 2D panels and the MFDs can be dragged: press and hold the left button on one, anywhere that is not a switch, button or slider, and move the mouse. The ICBM targeting range check now updates live as the target changes, and disarms if the target moves out of range.
- F5 steps the see-through HUD MFDs through both, left only and right only, and they can be dragged like the panel ones.
- Equipment may name a `ship` class: the released item becomes a real vessel (the ICBM warhead is one), stepped by the physics like a jettisoned stage, so the camera and the bus stay in step. The Arsenal bursts or burns it up.
- The external camera follows a released ejected warhead (the ICBM's) until it detonates (five seconds before the burst the camera eases out to 2 km, level with the warhead), while the bus makes a gentle separation burn.
- Default keys now follow Orbiter where they correspond: Keypad 2/8 pitch, 1/3 yaw, 4/6 bank, Keypad + full thrust, Keypad * cut thrust, Keypad 5 kills rotation (SAS), Keypad 0 / . hover thrust, Home / End hover on/off, Keypad 7 fires the primary and Insert the secondary weapons, `,` brakes, and T / R change the time-warp. The old WASD/QE/Z/X/B/L flight keys are gone. Key bindings now include Ctrl and Alt: Ctrl+Keypad +/- ramp the throttle, Ctrl+D undocks, Ctrl+I toggles the on-screen text, Ctrl+M opens the map, Ctrl+P pauses, Ctrl+C records video, Ctrl+Q quits and Alt+H changes the HUD colour. Keypad 5, `[`, `]`, `;` and `'` set the SAS to kill rotation, prograde, retrograde, normal and anti-normal (as Orbiter's navigation modes), so camera zoom moved to `-` and `=`. Addon `keys.json` actions can take `"ctrl"` and `"alt"`. Because R is now warp, restart moved to Shift+R, the EVA pack keys moved to the digit row, and SAS no longer uses T. Saved `keys.json` overrides keep their old bindings; reset them in the key settings to pick up the new defaults.
- New **field of view** keys, as in Orbiter: X widens and Z narrows it while held, and Ctrl+X / Ctrl+Z step through 10 to 90 degrees. It applies to every view until changed again.
- New **RCS linear mode**, as in Orbiter: Keypad / switches the RCS between rotation and linear, and Ctrl+Keypad / turns it off. In linear mode Keypad 2/8 push up/down, 1/3 left/right and 6/9 forward/back; Ctrl with any RCS key gives a tenth of the power. The HUD shows the mode (ROT, LIN or OFF). Keypad / no longer fires the primary weapon; it moved to Keypad 7, and the secondary to Insert.
- New **Dynamic Camera**: in the cockpit view the eye sits on springs, so it lags behind the ship under acceleration (thrust, pull-ups, landing thumps). Choose **View > Camera > Standard Camera** or **Dynamic Camera** (the default) and set its strength from 0 to 200%. Sustained g fades back to the seat. A ship can tune or disable it with a `cockpit.head_motion` block (`enabled`, `strength`, `frequency`, `damping`, `max`). The default spring is stiffer than it first was (3.8 Hz).
- New **re-entry plasma**: a ship falling faster than about 1.5 km/s through air glows by its heating, and its shape sets the look. A small warhead trails a thin faint streak, a capsule or the Deltaglider a medium glow, and a big blunt ship like the Shuttle-A a broad glowing ball; the colour runs from red-orange to white-violet as the heating rises. Every ship in the air shows it, with or without a `thermal` block.
- `--performance-window` opens it at start.
- New **ShipEdit** addon (Linux): a GTK 3 editor for ships and rocket stacks. Load any addon's ship, rearrange its stages, swap meshes (OBJ or GLB) or generated props per stage, edit masses, engines and ship properties with a live preview and per-stage delta-v and thrust-to-weight, then **Save** it back into its addon (the whole addon is backed up to `shipedit-backups/` first) or **Save as new addon**. Keys it does not show are kept as they were.
- A stage can start with a part-filled tank: `"fuel_start"` (0 to 1) in the stage, set in ShipEdit with a slider next to the propellant slider.
- New scenario 22, **Ares Spider, Gale Crater**: the Mars lander on the pad at Gale Crater base. Ships can set `"rcs_effects": false` to hide the generic RCS puffs (the Ares uses it), and spotlight glows now fade off the beam axis and stay small from far away.
- Internal cleanup with no change in behaviour: more long functions are split into smaller ones (ship landing and systems update, the power bus, addon loading, tagged-vessel discovery, terrain and clutter preparation, KosmosLink 3D overlays, the base editor's object tab, scenario start-up, body and world drawing, and ship control handling).
