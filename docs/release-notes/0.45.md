# Kosmos 0.45

- File ▸ Set time to T-5:00 before burn keeps re-planning for more passes, so long waits (Mars, Jupiter) end at the lead time instead of well short of it.
- The lead time for Set time to before burn is never less than five minutes. A lead of 0 skipped right onto the ignition, and the plan could then slip past it to the next window (days later).
- OrbitComp PLN warns in red when the active stage does not have the dV the plan needs ("NOT ENOUGH dV"), and AUTO will not arm for it. An outer-planet transfer such as Uranus needs several km/s more than a Deltaglider carries, so the burn ran dry and the ship fell far short.
- OrbitComp ORB and MAN keep your ship's dot on the display: the plot scales to include the ship (an escape or heliocentric path used to push it off the screen), and any marker still out of range is pinned to the edge of the plot.
- OrbitComp PLN ejections have a FAST button: choose FLIGHT or DEPART and adjust the flight time (10 days a press) or the departure date (1 day a press) with + and -. A Lambert arc about the star replaces the Hohmann ellipse, so a quick transfer to Jupiter or beyond can be planned, and TO MAN and AUTO work from it.
- OrbitComp MAN draws the Sun-centred arrival when the node's escape orbit meets the target: both planets' orbits, your path, and you and the target at closest approach. Adjusting the node moves the track closer to or farther from the target.
- OrbitComp's Lambert intercept (PLN, for eccentric or inclined orbits in one frame) failed with a script error and showed nothing; it works now.
- OrbitComp's FAST has a BEST mode: FIND searches the next synodic period for the departure date and flight time with the lowest departure speed, for a flight no longer than the one set.
- The MFD manual has a complete beginner's tutorial for OrbitComp (glossary, Hohmann and fast plans, checking the arrival on MAN, flying the burn, trimming, troubleshooting).
- KosmosCAD opens ships whose parts are glTF/GLB models (for example the Voyager addon). Before, it reported a GLB error and showed nothing; now the model loads as editable meshes, and saving writes them back as OBJ.
