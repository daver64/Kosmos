Saturn MLV stage specifications

The MLV-V-1, MLV-V-2, and MLV-V-3 stage masses, dimensions, and engine
performance in these vehicle files follow the stage data published by the
Encyclopedia Astronautica. This is more specific than the rounded propellant
figures in the Saturn MLV overview. The MLV-V-3 S-II mesh is the available
41-inch-stretch mesh, axially scaled to represent the 187-inch stretch.

The Saturn V-23L stage data follows the corresponding Encyclopedia
Astronautica entry. The four liquid boosters are represented as one separable
stage with aggregate mass and thrust values; each separated booster therefore
receives one quarter of the aggregate mass.

The MLV-V-1/NERVA entry is a visualization/performance approximation, not a
reconstruction of an MLV-specific NERVA configuration. Its engine and nuclear
stage mass use the generic NERVA values below; the MLV S-IVB model is retained
as a placeholder because a MLV-specific nuclear-stage mesh and tank definition
were not found.
