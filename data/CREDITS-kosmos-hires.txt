Kosmos high-resolution Earth imagery

The Earth surface tiles are Jestr's Earth Mark II virtual texture, based
largely on NASA Blue Marble imagery. Original tiles and the source readme
are included unchanged. Source: Celestia Motherlode, 32K Earth Mark II:
http://www.celestiamotherlode.net/addon/addon_61.html

The Celestia Motherlode states that files on the site are freely
distributable for non-commercial use, subject to the source creator's
terms: http://www.celestiamotherlode.net/legal.html
The Earth Mark II listing does not specify a more precise licence. This
pack is therefore distributed for non-commercial use only.

The Earth normal-map tiles are by Harald Schmidt, based on NASA Blue Marble
topography by Reto Stockli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. The original
tiles and readme are included unchanged. Celestia Motherlode identifies
this normal map as public domain:
https://www.celestiamotherlode.net/addon/addon_488.html
